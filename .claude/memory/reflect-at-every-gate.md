**Owner, verbatim:** "make sure to do these type of cleanup and reflections steps every time for
self-learning at planned gates such as at end of sessions or when finishing a plan, not just when i
ask you to reflect. Having issues repeat is a waste of time."

**Why:** the reflection that found this rule was only run because the owner asked. It turned up a
dozen repeatable costs from one session - a shell heredoc that had been silently joining GDScript
lines for weeks, review shots showing states no player can reach, tests green on either of two
product behaviours, a schedule change the owner learned about by asking. Each would have recurred.

**How to apply:** the procedure is `/handoff`'s "Reflect and record" section (list the costs from the
transcript, decide which recur, write each rule where it is read at that moment, prune the handoff,
say what was recorded in the last message). `/plan-run`'s close item 10 runs it over the whole run.
Do not wait to be asked; do not end a session without it.
