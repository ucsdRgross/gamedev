# Archive — the pre-grid design companions

These are the design documents **as they stood before the board became grids**. They describe the
two-zone tableau: an upper Entrance and a lower Ring, a whole-board **Submit** that scored and
cleared, three acts per show, and an act payout of `row_total × col_total × combo`. **None of that
exists.** The board is now one to three grids, a placement scores the lines it completes on the
spot, and there is no Submit and no act.

They are kept, not deleted, because the effect-review corpus
(`design/effect-review/`) was **mined from these files** and every drop and fold ruling in
`design/effect-review/DROPPED.csv` points back into them. Deleting them would strand that
provenance.

⚠ **Read the live version for anything you intend to build.** Read one of these only to resolve a
citation that predates the grid.

⚠ **Nothing in here is maintained.** They keep their own dated lines and their own superseded
timelines on purpose — that is what an archive is for — and `doc_check.py` does not scan this
directory as a living doc.
