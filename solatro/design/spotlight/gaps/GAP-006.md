## What actually happens, measured

From the owner's own playtest recording (`logs/events/playtest_1785851309/`), one submit:

```
f=9236  [input    ] submit
f=9236  [act      ] score_line — row index=0 cards=5
f=9236  [spotlight] section_changed — cards=5
f=9236  [light    ] dim_rising — target=0.750
f=9256  [act      ] score_line — row index=1 ...   <- 7 more sections, NO dim event
...
f=9341  [spotlight] section_changed — cards=0
f=9341  [light    ] dim_settled — at=0.000
```

**`dim_rising` once, `dim_settled` once, across EIGHT scored sections.** The dim goes up on the first
section and comes down only when the act releases. The owner's report is exact.

## Why it does that, and why it is nobody's decision

`QR2`=(d) is *"the dim is active if there are BEAMS"*, and `LightLayer._dim_target()` implements it
literally: non-empty light set → dim up. `Game._spotlight_section()` REPLACES the section's
membership without ever emptying it between sections (`Q16`=c — the forced spotlight travels and is
never torn down), so the light set is never empty mid-act, so the dim never falls.

⚠ **Every individual piece is correct and the composition was never checked.** `QR2`=(d) answered
*what drives the dim*; `Q16`=(c) answered *whether the forced set is torn down between sections*.
Neither asked what (d) IMPLIES when sections run back-to-back — and what it implies is a dim that is
up for the entire act, which is precisely the reading `QR2` was moved OFF of.

## Options I can see, in the questionnaire grammar

- **(a)** **The dim pulses per section.** `Game._spotlight_section()` emits an EMPTY set between
  sections (or the director retires on section end and re-lights on the next), so the dim falls and
  rises once per scored line. Consequence: matches `Q16`'s free text exactly and needs no new
  concept — the machinery already does this at act end. But eight sections means eight dim cycles in
  one submit, and at compressed act speed that could read as strobing; it also fights chart E, whose
  whole point is that a light TRAVELS between sections rather than dying and respawning.
- **(b)** **One dim for the act, as shipped** — `QR2`=(d) taken literally, `Q16`'s *"dims after
  initially showing"* withdrawn the way `Q102` and `Q74`–`Q76` were. Consequence: no code change, and
  it is the only option compatible with chart E's travelling light without qualification. But it is
  the state the owner has now described as wrong twice, once in the brief and once from a playtest.
- **(c)** **The dramatic opener** — chart S revived: dim up for the FIRST section only, falls once
  that line has scored, and the remaining lines score in normal light with beams and glow but no dim.
  Consequence: this is the literal *"fading away back to normal lit state for everything else"*
  quote, and it costs one dim cycle per act rather than eight. It needs `Q189` (what the last beat
  under the dim is), `Q190` (whether the lights survive the dim) and `Q192` (whether the column pass
  re-raises) — the three questions pruned when `QR2` settled, which would have to be answered.
- *my recommendation* **(c)**, and the measurement is the argument: **eight sections in ~1.8 s** in
  the owner's own recording. Option (a) would put eight full dim cycles into under two seconds, which
  is strobing rather than drama, and `Q167`'s dim fractions are fractions of `Game.get_delay()` so
  they compress with the act rather than resisting it. (c) keeps the dim a punctuation mark, which is
  what every one of the owner's descriptions of it has been.

## How it was implemented

**Visibility became an axis separate from the light SET, and that separation is the whole fix.**
`spotlight_section_changed` still answers *which cards are lit and where*; the new
`CardEnvironment.spotlight_reveal_ended` answers *is the show up*. `LightLayer._show` eases 0↔1 on
the same `Q167` fractions and multiplies BOTH the dim target and every light's intensity, so beams,
circles and dim fade as one show.

⚠ **The lights are NOT freed by the fade**, deliberately: they survive at their positions so the next
section can TRAVEL from them (chart E, *"no instant movements or spawning in and out"*). Fading to a
freed set would make chart E unbuildable on top of this.
⚠ **`_show` scales light INTENSITY, never the light COUNT** — dropping lights as they fade would read
as beams popping out of existence one at a time instead of dimming together.

**A second defect fell out of it, and only the trace could have shown it.** With the fade in, the log
showed `revealed` and `reveal_faded` on the **same frame** with `show=0.000`: nothing between them
ever waited, so the dim was easing toward a target it was already being pulled away from and never
left zero. **The hold beat had never been implemented** — chart D's **D13**, `Q68`=(a) *"yes, ~half a
delay"*, `PLAN.md` §1.11's `spotlight_hold_fraction`, default 0.5. Added, gated on `if view:` so
headless still waits on nothing (`Q19`=a) and G1.7's byte-identical parity holds.

Verified in `logs/events/spotlight_trace/`, per section, repeating:
`section_changed` → `revealed` → `dim_rising target=0.750` → `reveal_ended` → `reveal_faded
show=1.000` → `dim_settled at=0.000`. Suite `ALL 29 SUITES: 1779 CHECKS PASSED`, engine stream clean.
Guarded by `test_each_section_reveals_then_ends_its_reveal()` (3 checks: the reveal ends, it ends
AFTER its section was revealed, and exactly once per scored section).

⚠ **STILL UNTUNED AND IT MATTERS.** In the trace the whole cycle occupies 1–3 frames because
`get_delay()` is already compressed there (`dim_rising span=0.005s`). The mechanism is right; whether
it READS as a spotlight or as a flicker is a tuning question over `spotlight_hold_fraction`, the two
dim fractions and `base_delay` — and it is exactly what S18 exists to judge.
