# GAP-001 — `blocks_spotlight()` has two opposite polarities and the plan picked the wrong one

- **Raised:** 2026-08-04, executing `PLAN.md` phase 1, step **S3**.
- **ANSWERED by the owner in chat, 2026-08-04.** Implemented the same day.
- **Status:** answered, **awaiting fold-in.** `DESIGN.md` needs a version bump and `PLAN.md` §1.4
  is now known-wrong; neither has been edited (the plan is immutable, the design closes gaps).
- **Design nodes:** A8, `Q9`=(a), `Q6`=(a), U2, U3, U20. Plan §1.4.

## The answer, in the owner's words

> *"default is if card is covered by another card stacked on top, then that card is not active
> since its talent is hidden. Kuroko allows card it is on top of to be unhidden instead,
> activating its effect, and revealing allows the card it is attached to be spotlit anywhere even
> if card is covered."*

So the seam is the **GATE** reading, and the design's chart A8 was right all along:

| | |
|---|---|
| `blocks_spotlight()` is asked of | the card **doing the covering** |
| its default | **`true`** — a covering card hides the talent underneath it |
| Kuroko / Ghost Light | override it to **`false`**, unhiding the card beneath |
| `StampRevealing` | a property of the card **itself**, spotlit anywhere even when covered |
| a forced spotlight | bypasses all of it (`Q6`=a), unchanged |

This **replaces** `Game.is_data_topmost` in `is_spotlit()` rather than sitting in front of it: with
every modifier blocking by default, *"nothing above me"* and *"I am topmost"* are the same
statement, so shipped behaviour is unchanged.

## What was wrong, and where

- **`PLAN.md` §1.4 specifies `return false`.** With the gate reading that default makes **every
  covered card on the board spotlit**, because nothing would block anything. §1.4 is wrong and
  needs correcting when the plan is next revised. The line *"consults it before
  `is_data_topmost()`"* is also wrong: it consults it **instead of**.
- The original gap wrote the veto reading up as defensible on the strength of that default. It was
  not — it cannot express U20 (*"does not block spotlight beneath"*) at all, which is the one card
  the seam exists for.

## What shipped (`Cards/card_modifier.gd`)

```gdscript
if data.stamp is StampRevealing:
    return true
return not _blocked_from_above()
```

- `blocks_spotlight()` defaults to `true`.
- A card blocks unless **any one** of its modifiers opts out (`_card_blocks`) — one Kuroko stamp
  is enough; it does not have to convince the card's type and suit to agree.
- The walk stops at the first blocker, which (blocking being the default) is the card immediately
  above, so a covered card costs one comparison rather than a column scan.
- A zone/type header is blocked by any card in its column — the same rule `is_data_topmost`
  expressed for headers (*"topmost exactly when its column is empty"*).

Asserted by the SPOTLIGHT suite's `S3` section: default blocks, Kuroko unhides, Revealing is
self-owned and survives being buried two deep, headers follow their column, forced bypasses.
`Tests/Support/spotlight_test_kuroko.gd` is the only `blocks_spotlight() == false` in the tree;
real content on the seam stays out of scope (`Q185`=a).
