# GAP-002 — WITHDRAWN. Not a contradiction; the executor read one answer as two.

- **Raised:** 2026-08-04, executing `PLAN.md` phase 1, step **S5**.
- **WITHDRAWN 2026-08-04**, same day, after the owner pointed out there was nothing to decide.
- **Outcome:** no design change to the *behaviour*. `DESIGN.md` v7 rewords D20 and `Q16` so the
  same misreading is not available next time. The implementation was already correct and did not
  change.
- **Design nodes:** D10, D19, D20, D22, `Q16`. Plan §1.3, §1.5.

## What was filed

That `PLAN.md` §1.5 (`forced_spotlight = section.cards`, an assignment) contradicted `PLAN.md` §1.3
(*"`Q16` whole act"*) and design D20 (*"forced_spotlight stays set for the whole act"*) — and that
the difference was observable, so it had to be the owner's call.

## Why it was not a gap

> *"none of what I said contradicts each other. Forced spotlight stays for a whole act, moving from
> one section to another."*

**"Stays" and "moves" are one behaviour, not two competing ones.** The forced spotlight is never
torn down between sections — that is what makes a light *travel* instead of strobing, which is the
entire point of chart E — and its membership is whichever section is being scored. D20's "stays set
for the whole act" is about the light not going out; it was read as the *set* growing.

`Q16`'s answer said both halves plainly and the second one settles it:

> *"whole act? **increases or decreases based on cards being scored.** dims after initially showing,
> but gets revealed again at start of next scoring section, moves at same time as other row
> expanding and moves down with other cards moving down."*

*"Decreases"* is impossible under accumulation. The answer was never ambiguous.

## The actual failure, which is worth more than the gap was

**Every restatement of `Q16` dropped the operative half.** The answer is free text with no lettered
option; `PLAN.md` §1.3 compressed it to four words (*"`Q16` whole act"*) and D20 to a clause
(*"stays set for the whole act"*). Both kept "whole act" and both dropped "increases or decreases
based on cards being scored" — so an executor reading two lossy summaries of one answer saw a
conflict that does not exist in the source, and neither summary carried the source.

Two things follow, and they are in the v7 changelog:

1. **A free-text answer must be quoted, not paraphrased,** wherever a normative document depends on
   it. `Q16` now carries its note verbatim in §17.2, and D20 carries the operative clause inline.
2. **`Q16` gained a lettered option (c), "the whole act, TRAVELLING".** Its original (a)/(b) —
   *"only its line"* / *"the whole act, accumulating"* — did not contain the owner's answer, which
   is why it was answered in free text at all. An answer that had to be written as prose because
   no option fitted is a question that needs a new option, and round 1 did that for `QR2`=(d) and
   `Q24`=(c) but never for `Q16`.

**Nothing here was a decision. Filing it as a gap cost the owner a round trip, which is the exact
cost the gap protocol exists to avoid** — and the protocol's own rule 3 ("the design contradicts
itself → always a gap") is what made it look correct. Rule 3 needs the qualifier that a
contradiction between two *restatements* is a documentation bug to be resolved against the source
answer, not a decision to escalate.
