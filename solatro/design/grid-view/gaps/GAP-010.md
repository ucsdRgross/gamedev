# GAP-010 — The Entrance must be PINNED and independently scrollable, which the design did not ask about

status: open
outcome: —
raised: 2026-08-28, during S20b.3 (attempted and backed out)
design: grid-view DESIGN.md v2 charts `L1`, `L2`, `L4`; `QR3`=(a); `Q21`=(a)
severity: GAP

## OWNER INSTRUCTION that produced this

> *"Entrance cards should always remain visible at bottom even when scrolling up or down a grid so
> that cards are always abled to be picked up and placed anywhere in grid. since entrance has its
> own stacking, also need entrance to be scollable as well independent of grid scrolling, but
> still maintain x movement and attaching."*

## What the design says

`QR3`=(a) and chart `L2` already say the Entrance *"belongs to the board, not to any one grid, and
stays on screen wherever the player has panned"*, and `L4` says a stacked Entrance slot draws with
the same strips as a grid cell. So **"always visible" is already the approved design** — it was
simply not built that way: the Entrance was left inside the board's own scroll container, which
satisfies `L1`'s *"one strip across the bottom"* and not `L2`'s *"stays on screen"*.

## What it does not say

Three things, and each is a real decision:

1. **Its own scrolling.** The Entrance stacks (`Q21`=a), so a deep slot needs somewhere to go. The
   design never asked whether the Entrance scrolls, or how that interacts with the board's scroll.
2. **What "maintain x movement and attaching" binds to.** The Entrance's slots line up with a
   grid's columns (`L3`, and `TP-80k` now pins it). If the board pans horizontally, the Entrance
   must follow in x while staying pinned in y — so it is neither fully pinned nor fully scrolled,
   and which of the two axes couples to the board is a contract.
3. **Which grid it attaches to** once the board can pan between several, given it is one strip for
   the whole picture.

## What the repo says — measured, by attempting it

The attempt is backed out; the suite is green. What it established is worth keeping, because none
of it is guessable from the outside:

- **`%CardLayer` lives INSIDE the scroll container** (`play_area.tscn`), so pinning the Entrance's
  ROW pins nothing: its cards scroll away from their own controls. The Entrance needs **its own
  card layer** outside the scroll.
- **Draw ordering is per-layer.** `_order_board_cards` moves children to an index, and an index
  only means anything inside the layer that holds them. Ordering two layers against one crashes,
  and — worse — treats every visual in the other layer as a deferred add that never lands, which
  re-queues the reorder every frame until the stack overflows. Measured.
- **A pooled visual does not follow its card between layers.** A card placed out of the Entrance
  onto a grid keeps drawing in the pinned strip unless `_bind_slot` reparents it.
- ⚠ **`slot_center_global` STOPS MIRRORING THE CONTAINER the moment the row is centred.** Its
  formula starts at `hbox.global_position.x`; with `ALIGNMENT_CENTER` the children start at an
  offset inside the hbox, and the math and the controls disagree by half the slack (measured: 20
  px). Either the row is left-aligned and padded, or the formula learns about the alignment.
- **The blast radius is the SUITES, not the product**: 42 checks failed, 34 of them in
  `VISUAL LAYERS`, which builds its boards in `upper_zone` and asserts geometry against the legacy
  zone container. That is the same rebuild `S20b.4` already owns.

## Why it matters

The Entrance is the only place a card can be picked up, and a placement can target any cell of any
grid. On a board taller than the window — which is every board at the old `card_scale`, and any
multi-grid board at the new one — an Entrance that scrolls with the grid means the player scrolls
to the cell they want and no longer has a card to put in it. It is not a polish item; it is the
loop not working.

## Options I can see

- **(a)** Entrance pinned in y, its x offset SLAVED to the board's horizontal scroll, with its own
  vertical scroll for its stacks — the literal reading of the instruction. The strip never moves
  vertically, its slots stay under their columns as the board pans, and a deep slot scrolls
  internally · **(b)** as (a), but the Entrance's vertical scroll is COUPLED to nothing and it
  simply grows upward into the board area when it stacks, overlapping the grid — fewer moving
  parts, no second scrollbar, at the cost of the Entrance covering the bottom row · **(c)** the
  Entrance is pinned and does NOT scroll at all; a stack deeper than the strip fans or compresses
  instead — no second scroll container anywhere, but it contradicts `Q21`=(a) · *my recommendation*
  (a), because it is what was asked and the other two each drop one half of it

⚠ **Whichever wins, it should land WITH `S20b.4`, not before it.** The 34 `VISUAL LAYERS` failures
are not caused by the pinning — they are the legacy-zone fixtures meeting a board that no longer
renders zones, which is exactly what `S20b.4` is for. Doing the pinning first means rebuilding
those fixtures twice.

**Blast radius** — plan steps `S20b.3`, `S20b.4`; design nodes `L1`, `L2`, `L4`, `J9`, `M1`;
`UI/play_area.gd` (`_bind_slot`, `_order_board_cards`, `slot_center_global`, `set_card_zones`),
`UI/play_area.tscn`, and `Tests/UI/test_visual_layers.gd`.

**Meanwhile** — parked: the pinning itself, and `S20b.3`. Continued on: everything the attempt
did NOT depend on — `card_scale` 1.0 and the two tests that were only correct at 2.5, both landed
