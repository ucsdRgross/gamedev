## Was it in the design and plan?

**Half of it.** Chart `P1`/`P2` retires the zone rendering and `S20b.4` says *"rebuild the fixtures
onto grids"*, so the destination is written down. Two things are NOT:

1. **No document states the RULE** — that every behaviour the zone renderer has must have a grid
   equivalent, and every assertion about a zone must have a grid twin or an explicit retirement.
   `GAP-009` said the grid view *replaces* the play area; what was built is a grid renderer
   ALONGSIDE the zone one, and nothing checks the two for parity.
2. **The SEQUENCING puts the live renderer before its tests.** `S20b.1`/`.2` shipped the grid as
   the board the player uses; the fixtures do not move until `S20b.4`. Everything in between is a
   window where the grid is live and untested for layering. This bug lived in that window.

⚠ **The coverage briefly existed and was reverted.** `GAP-010` records that the fixture rebuild was
attempted and WORKED — *"`VISUAL LAYERS` went from 32 failures to 1"* — then was backed out with
the Entrance pinning. So the tests that would have caught this were written and then removed, and
no marker was left saying the coverage had gone.

## Options I can see

- **(a)** Keep `GAP-010`'s sequencing and add a **parity ratchet now**: a gate that records the
  count of zone-only test files and fails if it ever rises, plus a written inventory naming, for
  each zone behaviour, its grid twin or its retirement. Coverage still lands at `S20b.4`, but no
  new hole can open and the existing ones are enumerated rather than discovered by eye · **(b)**
  **Bring `S20b.4` forward** — delete the legacy zone rendering BEFORE `S20b.2b`, so a zone-only
  test cannot compile and the parity question answers itself. ⚠ This CONTRADICTS `GAP-010`, whose
  answer was that the Entrance pinning and the zone deletion land together after the coordinate
  migration, precisely because three renderers at once produced 1041 engine errors · **(c)** Port
  the eleven zone-only suites to grids NOW as their own step, ahead of both, leaving the product
  sequencing untouched · *my recommendation* **(a) plus (c) for `test_visual_layers.gd` alone** —
  the ratchet is cheap and stops the bleeding immediately, and the layering suite is the one whose
  absence is actively dangerous, while porting the other ten is work `S20b.4` will redo anyway
