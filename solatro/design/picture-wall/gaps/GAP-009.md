# GAP-009 — rings are rejected as the model; continuous angular placement needs its rules written
status: open
raised: 2026-08-15, superseding GAP-006, before execution plan step S4 (`WallPacker`)
design: DESIGN.md version 5, nodes G2, G3, G4; PLAN.md §1.1, §1.3; TEST_PLAN.md P2, P3, P4
severity: CONTRADICTION

**What the design says** — the whole packer is written in rings. `G2`: *"Ring capacity is computed"*
(`DESIGN.md:632`). `G3`: *"Position within a ring is an authored slot index"* (`DESIGN.md:633`).
`G4`: *"A partial ring stays at its authored angles"* (`DESIGN.md:634`). `PLAN.md` §1.1 gives
`PictureEntry` an authored `ring : int` and `slot : int`; §1.3 rules 3 and 4 fill rings and order
within them.

**What the ANSWER says** — the owner, answering GAP-006:

> "I think the framing of it as rings is actually bad, since a large frame could theoretically
> overlap multiple rings. Its more like continuously placing around a center point at angles in a
> star pattern but not literally a star pattern"

Overflow is confirmed as the packing behaviour, but the **container** it overflows between is
rejected. The argument is concrete and correct: picture size is free (`size_multiplier`) and frame
thickness is per-side (`frame_px`), so one picture can span several ring-bands radially. A single
shared radius per ring cannot describe that, and any ring assignment for such a picture is a lie.

**What it does not say** — **the placement rule that replaces rings.** "Continuously around a centre
point at angles" fixes the shape of the answer and not its content. At minimum:

1. **What sets each picture's angle?** The authored `slot` reinterpreted as an angle or an order? An
   even distribution computed from the count? A fixed irrational step (a sunflower/golden-angle
   placement, which is the usual way to get "star-like but not literally a star")?
2. **What sets each picture's radius?** The smallest radius at which it clears everything already
   placed plus `gap_px`, evaluated against real frame rectangles rather than a shared band?
3. **What is the placement ORDER?** Order changes the result in any greedy packing: `slot`
   ascending, largest-first, or unlock order?
4. **Do `ring` and `slot` survive on `PictureEntry`?** `ring` is shipped (S3) and would become
   meaningless; `slot` may survive as pure ordering. Removing `ring` is a resource-format change.
5. **Does the ellipse still apply?** `Q10`=(c) clamps an ellipse *aspect*. Under continuous
   placement that most naturally becomes an anisotropic scale on the whole arrangement — but that is
   an inference, not a decision.

**Why it blocks** — triage test (3), the design contradicting itself, now compounded: the design,
the plan and three planned tests all describe a model the owner has rejected. Building the packer
against either model without this answered guarantees a rewrite. It is also expensive to reverse —
`WallPacker` is consumed by S10, S36 and the S34 tool.

⚠ **It has already invalidated shipped work.** `PictureEntry.ring` landed in **S3** (commit
`b5006a9`) and is now probably wrong. That is cheap to change *now* and expensive once
`layout_default.tres` is authored against it.

**Options I can see** — **(a)** **sunflower / golden-angle placement**: each picture takes the next
golden-angle step around the centre and the smallest radius that clears every placed frame plus
`gap_px`; `ring` is deleted, `slot` becomes pure placement order. Gives the "star-like but not a
star" look directly, is deterministic per `Q18`=(a), and handles any size mix without special cases;
the authored control that `G3` wanted is reduced to ordering only · **(b)** **authored angle,
computed radius**: `slot` is reinterpreted as an authored angle around the centre — fixed art
direction as `G3` intended — and only the radius is best-fit; `ring` is deleted. Keeps authored
control and keeps `G4`'s "visibly unfinished" partial arrangement meaningful; needs an authored
angle per picture, so the layout carries more numbers · **(c)** **keep `ring` as a coarse grouping
hint only**, with continuous radius within it — least disruptive to shipped code and to P2/P3/P4,
but retains the abstraction the owner just called bad and still cannot describe a picture spanning
two bands · *my recommendation* **(b)**, because `Q12`=(a) and `G3` chose authored positions
deliberately — *"the same on every player's wall, fixed art direction"* — and (a) silently discards
that; (b) is the smallest change that honours both the owner's geometry and the answered questions

**Blast radius** — `PLAN.md` §1.1 (`ring`, `slot`), §1.3 (rules 3, 4); `DESIGN.md` G2, G3, G4;
`TEST_PLAN.md` **P2, P3, P4 must be rewritten or withdrawn** — they assert ring membership and
per-ring counts, which no longer exist under (a) or (b); steps S4, S10, S36, S34; the shipped
`PictureEntry` resource format.

**Meanwhile** — parked: S4 and everything consuming the packer's geometry (S10, S36, S34). Continued
on: **S9** (`wall.tscn` skeleton), which needs no packer, plus the remaining doc amendments and S2's
second reading. S3's `ring` field is left in place rather than pre-emptively removed — removing it
before the model is chosen would be inventing the answer.
