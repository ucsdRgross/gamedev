## OWNER ANSWER — Q48 becomes (a), and Q49 is (b)

**Zoom out only far enough to show the SOURCE picture's own frame plus the margin.** Travel covers
the distance; the zoom no longer expresses it. Duration stays FIXED regardless of distance
(`Q49`=b), so a far jump reads as a longer slide at close zoom rather than a bigger zoom.

⚠ **This is a GATE flip and it moves two other answers.** `Q50` (`[Q48=b]`, "should travel duration
*also* scale?", answered (a) no) is STRANDED — it can never be asked again on this path. `Q49`
(`[Q48=a|c]`) was never active and is now answered. The observable behaviour is unchanged by that
pair: both said "fixed duration", so nothing about timing moved.

## The question

`Q48`=b was chosen in round 1 and chart C8 states it: *"Zoom out far enough to show BOTH frames.
Q48=b — distance decides the zoom, so a far jump reads as a journey."* Implemented faithfully in
`WallTransition._wide_zoom()`.

Seen in the tool, a far jump zooms out to most of the wall, which reads as being thrown out to the
overview and back rather than as leaving a picture. `Q48`'s own option (a) is the correction,
verbatim: *"exactly far enough to show the source picture's whole frame plus a small margin."*

## What changed

`test_wall_transition.gd`'s two T4 rows asserted the old contract and now assert the new one,
including that the plateau is IDENTICAL whether the destination is small or huge.
