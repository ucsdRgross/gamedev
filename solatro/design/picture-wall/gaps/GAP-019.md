# GAP-019 — what does the camera do DURING a reduced-motion cross-fade?

status: open
outcome: awaiting owner
raised: fixing the reduced-motion resting-zoom defect
design: `Q172`=a; `Q173`=a; K8; T12; H3/`Q27`/S37; PICTURE_WALL.md C2
severity: GAP

## The question

`Q172`=a says reduced motion "replaces every transition with a **cross-fade at a fixed zoom**", and
T12 pins that as "camera zoom is constant for the whole transition". H3/`Q27` say a focused picture
**overfills its window, so no frame is ever visible at rest**. Those two cannot both hold across a
move whose source and destination have different focused zooms — a constant zoom cannot equal two
different resting values. Something has to give, and where it gives is visible.

- **(a) Fixed at `_wide_zoom` (the "show both frames" framing), settle to the destination at the
  end.** Zoom snaps out at the start of the move, holds while the two screens cross-fade and the
  camera slides, then snaps in on landing. **This is what is implemented now.** Two snaps and a
  slide per move.
- **(b) Fixed at the DESTINATION's own focused zoom.** One snap, at the start; the cross-fade then
  happens already framed on where you are going, and landing is continuous. The outgoing screen is
  hugely magnified while it fades, which may read as a zoom the player did not ask for.
- **(c) No camera move at all.** The camera stays exactly where it is, the two screens cross-fade in
  place, and the camera cuts to the destination's resting pose when the fade completes. One cut, no
  slide, no zoom — the least motion of the three, and the closest to what a reduced-motion setting
  is usually for. It reads `Q173`=a's "reached by cross-fade rather than by zooming" literally, but
  drops the travel that PICTURE_WALL.md C2 ("reduced motion still ARRIVES at the destination") and
  the current code comment both take `Q172` to require.

## Why it is a gap and not a judgement call

All three satisfy the letter of "a cross-fade at a fixed zoom" and all three differ in what the
player sees on every single navigation. The choice is about motion comfort for a player who enabled
this setting *because* motion is a problem for them — which is an owner call about the audience, not
a reading of the source.

## What is NOT in dispute, and is already fixed

Every option ends with the camera at the destination's resting pose. It did not: `sample_at()`'s
reduced branch returns `_wide_zoom` for every elapsed *including the last*, and nothing re-posed the
camera afterwards, so every destination came to rest at ~0.44 zoom instead of ~1.0 with its own
frame plainly visible — the exact state H3 exists to forbid, on every transition. `Main`
`_focus_picture()` now calls `_settle_camera()` when a move lands, which is the same resting pose a
resize settles to. That fix stands whichever option is chosen here.

## What is reversible about it

Cheaply, and in one place: the reduced branch of `WallTransition.sample_at()` is four lines, and T12
is its test. No data, no authored resource, no other call site.
