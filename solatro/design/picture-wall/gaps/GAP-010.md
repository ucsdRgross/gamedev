# GAP-010 — rebalancing lopsided arrangements reverses Q13=(b), which chose to keep authored angles
status: resolved
outcome: answered

## AMENDED — rebalancing is UNCONDITIONAL, including full unlock

> "walls should always rebalance to deal with switching between landscape and portrait modes."

The first implementation made full unlock the **identity** (authored angles verbatim) and rebalanced
only partial sets, to keep `slot`'s authored angle meaningful. The snapshot proved that reading
wrong: `01_wall_view_everything_unlocked` was still visibly lopsided. **Rebalancing now applies to
every unlock set, complete or not.**

Consequence, accepted: **`slot` decays to placement ORDER, not an authored angle.** `Q12`=(a) and
`G3`'s *"the same on every player's wall, fixed art direction"* survive only in the weak sense that
the arrangement is a deterministic function of the unlocked set. GAP-009's option (b) — "authored
angle, computed radius" — is effectively superseded by its option (a) in all but ordering.

⚠ **The stated reason names a mode the design does not support.** `G13` fixes the aspect range as
*"4:3 to 32:9"* — all landscape — and `WallLayout.ellipse_aspect_min` is **1.2**, so a portrait
window (aspect < 1.0) clamps to 1.2 and the ellipse stays landscape while the window is tall.
Rebalancing helps distribute pictures but **does not make portrait work**; that needs `G13`'s range
and the clamp floor changed, and it is not in any step. Flagged, not assumed.

## Owner's answer — (b) rebalance; "snapping in place"

> "yes think of it as snapping in place."

**Authored angles become starting positions, not final ones.** The packer redistributes to reduce
lopsidedness and each picture *snaps* to its resolved position. This was raised twice as reversing
`Q13`=(b) and confirmed both times, so it is a deliberate override of that answer.

**Consequences, accepted:**

- **`Q13`=(b) and `G4` are withdrawn.** The "visibly unfinished" partial wall is gone.
- ⚠ **The unlock signal it carried is gone with it.** `Q13`=(b) was chosen because an unfinished
  wall *"telegraphs that there is more to unlock"*. A rebalanced wall always looks composed, so
  nothing tells the player more exists. **No replacement signal is specified anywhere** — that is a
  real hole, and a later design pass owes it an answer.
- **`G3`'s "the same on every player's wall"** now holds only for identical unlock sets, since the
  arrangement is a function of which pictures are present.
- **P4′ is now WRONG and green** — it asserts angles are "NOT redistributed to close the gaps",
  which is exactly the behaviour being replaced. It must be rewritten, not deleted.
- Determinism (`Q18`=a) still binds: same inputs, same output, always. Snapping must be a pure
  function of the unlocked set, not of arrival order or animation state.

**What the design says** — `G4`: *"A partial ring stays at its authored angles and reads as visibly
unfinished. Q13=b"* (`DESIGN.md:634`). `G3`: *"Position within a ring is an authored slot index.
Q12=a — the same on every player's wall, fixed art direction"* (`DESIGN.md:633`). GAP-009's
resolution carried both forward: the angle is **authored**, only the radius is computed.

**What the ANSWER says** — `Q13` offered exactly this choice and the owner rejected it:
**(a)** *"the occupied slots spread evenly around the full ring, so gaps are equal and large"* was
declined in favour of **(b)** *"the occupied slots stay adjacent at their authored angles and the
ring is visibly partial — the wall looks unfinished, which telegraphs that there is more to
unlock"* (`DESIGN.md:902`). The unfinished look was the **point**, not a side effect.

**What the owner now says**, seeing S10's snapshot:

> "ideally it shouldnt be lopsided, it should attempt rearranging existing to reduce lopsidedness if
> possible by spreading radial out more"

**What it does not say** — whether this **overrides `Q13`=(b)**. Lopsidedness is an angular property:
the only way to reduce it is to move pictures off their authored angles, which is `Q13`=(a), the
option that was declined. The request may instead be aimed at the *fixture* — the snapshot's six
pictures were authored at clustered angles, so the wall would look lopsided under any rule. Those two
readings are indistinguishable from the image.

**Why it blocks** — triage test (2) and (3): it reverses an explicit owner answer, and the two
readings differ visibly on every wall a player sees. It also **invalidates a test that is already
green** — `TEST_PLAN.md` P4′ asserts that authored angles survive partial unlock and are *"NOT
redistributed to close the gaps"*, which is `Q13`=(b) expressed as an assertion. Under a rebalancing
rule that test is wrong, not merely incomplete.

⚠ **`Q13`=(b) is load-bearing for a gameplay signal, not just a look.** The unfinished wall is how
the player is told there is more to unlock. Rebalancing to fill the space removes that signal, and
nothing else in the design replaces it.

**Options I can see** — **(a)** **fixture only** — the placement rule is unchanged and the snapshot
is re-authored with angles spread around the full circle, plus square pictures for variety; costs
nothing, keeps `Q13`=(b) and P4′ intact, and tests whether the lopsidedness was ever real ·
**(b)** **rebalance always** — authored angles become hints and the packer redistributes to minimise
lopsidedness; the wall always looks composed, `Q13`=(b) and `G3`'s "same on every player's wall" are
withdrawn, P4′ is rewritten, and the unlock signal needs a replacement · **(c)** **rebalance only
when badly lopsided** — authored angles are honoured until some measured imbalance threshold is
crossed, then spread; keeps most of the art direction and still fills egregious holes, at the cost of
a new authored threshold and a wall whose layout changes discontinuously as pictures unlock ·
*my recommendation* **(a) first**, because it is free and answers the question the snapshot actually
raised — if a wall with well-spread authored angles still reads as lopsided, then (b) or (c) is
warranted and we will know it rather than guessing

**Blast radius** — `DESIGN.md` G3, G4, `Q13`; GAP-009's resolution; `WallPacker`'s placement order;

any threshold (c) introduces.
