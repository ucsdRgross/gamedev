# GAP-016 — WallOverlay covers screen controls, and nothing fixes where it may sit
status: open
raised: 2026-08-17, after the `main.gd` wire-up put real screens behind the overlay
design: DESIGN.md version 5, nodes B8, F7, F8, F9; PLAN.md §2 S35
severity: GAP

**What the design says** — `B8` and `S35` establish `WallOverlay` as *"the persistent `CanvasLayer`
carrying Back, Forward, Wall and the top-right Info toggle"*. `Q65`=(c) requires an unavailable
control to *look* unavailable. Nothing anywhere says where these controls sit, or what happens when
a screen's own UI is already there.

**What the ANSWER says** — the `F`-chart questions fix what the controls DO and what they look like
when disabled. No question was ever asked about their placement, so there is no answer to consult.

**What it does not say** — **where the overlay may and may not sit over a screen.** It is a
persistent `CanvasLayer` above every picture, so it is not clipped by the picture and cannot be
avoided by a screen that does not know it exists.

**Measured collisions** at 1280×720. Overlay Back/Forward/Wall occupy window-space **x 12–268,
y 672–708**:

| Screen | Result |
|---|---|
| `start_menu` | **collides** — "Profile" (~x16–203, y577–663) and "Options" (~x16–203, y572–659) sit directly behind Back and Forward |
| `map` | **collides** — `DeckButton` (~x13–124, y662–707) overlaps Back almost exactly |
| `game` | clear — nearest bottom controls end at ~y651, above the overlay band |
| `deck` | no live screen yet, nothing to collide |
| top-right `InfoButton` | clear on every screen |

**Why it blocks** — triage test (2), and it is an owner call about look and layout, which the
protocol reserves. It is also **not fixable inside any one screen**: `Q211`=(a) and anti-scope item 1
forbid redesigning screen layouts, so the two colliding screens cannot legally move their own
buttons out of the way. The fix has to be the overlay's, and choosing it is art direction.

⚠ **The consequence is worse than cosmetic.** Two controls stacked in the same place means a click
lands on whichever is on top — the overlay — so `start_menu`'s Profile and Options and the map's
Deck button are **effectively unreachable by mouse** while the overlay is visible. A player cannot
open their profile. That makes this a functional defect wearing a layout problem's clothes.

**Options I can see** — **(a)** move the overlay to a band no screen uses — the top-left corner is
free on all four screens, and the Info toggle already lives top-right unopposed; cheapest, needs no
screen changes, but splits the controls across two corners unless Info moves too · **(b)** give the
overlay its own reserved gutter outside the picture — the picture is inset by the gutter's height
so nothing can ever sit underneath; guarantees no collision on any current or future screen, at the
cost of the picture no longer filling the window, which contradicts `H3`'s overfill-at-rest and
would make a frame edge visible · **(c)** auto-hide the overlay while a screen is focused, showing
it on hover or on a modifier — keeps the full-bleed picture and the current corner, but hides
navigation exactly when a player most needs it, and `Q65`=(c)'s "unavailable controls must look
unavailable" implies controls are meant to be visible · *my recommendation* **(a)** — it is the only
option that changes no screen, breaks no answered question, and costs nothing but a corner

**Blast radius** — `UI/Wall/wall_overlay.tscn`'s anchors; `start_menu` and `map` interaction;
`TEST_PLAN.md` F8/F9 assert disabled-ness, not position, so no planned test constrains the answer.
S34's tool does not expose overlay placement, and may need to.

**Meanwhile** — parked: the overlay's placement. Continued on: everything else. The overlay stays
where it is, colliding, until this is answered — recorded rather than nudged, because moving it on
implementer judgement would bake an art-direction decision nobody made.
