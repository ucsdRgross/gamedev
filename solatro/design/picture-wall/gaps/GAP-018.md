# GAP-018 — is `WallLayout.view_margin` extra crop, or a leftover from Q5's rejected option?

status: resolved
outcome: answered — option (a)
raised: during the M9 pass (PICTURE_WALL.md, "five knobs nothing reads")
design: DESIGN.md §5 tunables; `Q5`; GAP-008; PLAN.md §1.2; G9
severity: GAP

## OWNER ANSWER — option (a)

`view_margin` **is extra crop**, as its own field comment says. It stays wired as it is; nothing to
change. The wall-view framing question it feeds (four of twelve pictures cut at 16:9) stays open in
`todo.md` as a composition call, separate from what this field MEANS.

## The question

`WallLayout.view_margin` (0.06) had **no reader at all** — `Wall.wall_view_zoom()` used
`wall_overfill_margin` instead, which is a *picture* knob (H3/GAP-011) about the focused picture's
own overfill, not the wall's framing. So the wall's crop bias was whatever the picture knob
happened to be.

Wiring it up needs a reading of what it means, and there are two, differing observably:

- **(a) It is extra crop, as its own field comment says** (`## Q5=b fill, this is the crop bias`).
  Wall view zooms in `1 + view_margin` past the plain fill, so the outermost frames sit further
  outside the window. **This is what is implemented now.**
- **(b) It is vestigial.** `Q5`'s option **(c)** — the default the owner did *not* pick — was "fit
  with a margin percentage so there is always a little wall visible around the outermost frames".
  `view_margin` is that percentage. `Q5`=(b) chose plain fill, which needs no margin, so on this
  reading the field should be struck exactly as GAP-008 struck four `PlayerSettings` rows.

## Why it is a gap and not a judgement call

Both readings are defensible from the source, and they differ in something the owner has already
said they care about: the run's own handoff flags that **wall view never shows the whole wall**
(at 16:9, 4 of 12 pictures are cut by the frame; more at 32:9) and asks for an owner look before
Phase 6 builds on it. Option (a) makes that *more* pronounced; option (b) leaves it as it is today.

## What is reversible about it

Cheaply. `view_margin` lives on `layout_default.tres`, which the S34 tool edits — **setting it to
`0.0` restores exactly the framing that shipped before this change**, with no code edit. Choosing
(b) instead means deleting the field, its `PLAN.md` §1.2 row and the `DESIGN.md` §5 row, and
reverting `wall_view_zoom()` to a plain fill.

## Not affected either way

The margin is applied **through** `WallPicture.focused_scale()`, so it stays conditional on the
wall's aspect differing from the window's (H3/DEFECT 1). At a matching aspect fill and fit still
coincide exactly, which is what keeps G10's "panning is off when everything is visible" an exact
zero rather than a stray few per cent of slack.
