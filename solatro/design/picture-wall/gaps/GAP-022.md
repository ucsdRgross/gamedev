# GAP-022 — Info mode must cover nothing, neither by the window nor by the card

status: resolved
outcome: answered — Q128 re-answered; new knob `wall_info_card_overlap`
raised: owner playtest through `Tools/wall_editor.tscn`
design: `Q128`; chart J2, J3, J4
severity: GAP

## OWNER ANSWER

**Info mode zooms OUT until the whole screen fits ABOVE the card.** The reason the view moves at
all is so nothing is covered, so the card must not cover the screen either — except a tunable
overlap at the bottom, which keeps the card reading as something sitting IN FRONT of the picture
rather than a band beside it.

## The question

Two things were wrong at once.

**The implementation contradicted the chart.** J2 says *"**Zooms out** just enough to reveal the
BOTTOM frame only"*; `WallPicture.info_zoom_state()` PANNED the camera down at an unchanged zoom.
A pan drags the top of the visible rect down with it, so the top of the screen was cropped —
content lost behind the window edge with no way to reach it, in the one mode whose purpose is
reading the screen.

**The answer itself needed revisiting.** `Q128`'s round-1 free text was *"Enough to show bottom
frame, regardless of top right left sides. This means top should remain covered so we dont zoom out
too much"* — written before anyone had seen it. `Q128`'s own option (b), the default it overrode,
is close to the new answer: *"further, so the card has room below the picture without covering it."*

## What changed

`info_zoom_state()` reserves the card out of the window first, fits the picture into what is left,
and shifts the camera down by half the reserve so the picture sits in the free region above.
New knob `wall_info_card_overlap` (screen px, default 24) is how much the card may cover.

⚠ **The reserve is the card's LIVE height, not `wall_info_card_max_height`.** Reserving the cap was
tried first and read as being thrown out to the wall: a two-line caption pulled the camera back as
far as the longest description would. `info_zoom_state()` takes a `card_height_px`; callers with a
card on screen pass its real height, and the cap is only the fallback for callers that have none
(the transition's own info branch). A taller entry re-settles the camera, and an equal-height one
does not, so hovering does not nudge it.

## Round 3 — the transition into/out of an info-mode move snapped at both ends

Two separate causes, both now fixed:

- **`sample_at()` computed its info pose without the card height**, so it reserved the authored CAP
  while the resting pose reserved the card's LIVE height. The camera jumped the instant a move
  began. `sample_at()` and `request()` now take `card_height_px`, frozen with the settings so the
  pose cannot change shape mid-move.
- **The info branch held the SOURCE's zoom for the whole move.** A differently-sized destination was
  therefore reached at the wrong zoom, and `_settle_camera()` cut to the right one on landing.
  Position AND zoom now interpolate between the two real info poses, on the authored travel curve.

⚠ `J10`/`Q137` — *"with info mode on a transition is a pure travel, the camera never leaves the info
zoom"* — is honoured by staying BETWEEN the two info poses the whole way, which is what
`TestWallInfo` now asserts. Its previous row REQUIRED a constant zoom, i.e. required the snap; that
row was rewritten, not relaxed.

## Round 4 — the move aimed at the wrong pose, and Info mode became per screen

- **A move computed its target from `focused_scale()` even with Info mode on**, so it zoomed into
  the ordinary focused pose and the settle then CUT to the info pose — the camera arriving as if
  Info mode were not on until the instant it landed. `WallPicture.resting_state()` is now the one
  answer to "where does the camera rest on this picture", info-aware, and every mover aims at it.
- **Info mode is now PER PICTURE** (owner ruling). The map can be left in info mode while the board
  is not. `wall_info_mode` on the settings is only ever the FOCUSED picture's entry, written on
  every focus change, so nothing downstream had to learn that the mode is per screen.
  ⚠ A toggle made MID-MOVE is a choice about the DESTINATION, not the picture being left — without
  that, arriving restored the destination's old state and silently dropped what the player chose,
  which `TestWallPause` already pinned.

## Blast radius

`WallPicture.info_zoom_state()`, `PlayerSettings.wall_info_card_overlap`, chart J2, and every
caller of the info pose — `Main._settle_camera()`, `Main._on_info_toggled()`,
`WallTransition.sample_at()`'s info branch, and the wall editor's own pose.
