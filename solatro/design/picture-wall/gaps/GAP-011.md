# GAP-011 — the overfill margin is a tunable typed into a `.gd`, which §1.8 forbids
status: open
raised: 2026-08-15, during execution plan step S37
design: PLAN.md §1.8, §2 S37; DESIGN.md §5, node H3
severity: GAP

**What the design says** — `PLAN.md` §1.8 is unambiguous: *"No number in this section is a constant.
Every value an author could reasonably argue with is either a `PlayerSettings` knob (`DESIGN.md` §5)
or a field on `WallLayout` / `PictureEntry`… If you find yourself typing a literal into a `.gd` file
that is not in one of those three places, stop — that is a gap."* S37 implements H3: the focused
picture overfills the window whenever its aspect does not match, so no frame is visible at rest.

**What the ANSWER says** — nothing fixes the *amount* of overfill. `Q32`/`H2` fix `keep_aspect`,
`H3` fixes that overfill happens. No question asks how much.

**What it does not say** — **how far past the window edge a focused picture reaches.** S37 shipped
`_OVERFILL_MARGIN = 1.02` in `UI/Wall/wall_picture.gd`: a 2 % bleed. It was categorised alongside
`wall_packer.gd`'s `_EPS`, but the two are different in kind. `_EPS` (1e-6) is a floating-point
guard with no defensible alternative value. **2 % is a visible design choice** — an author could
reasonably argue for 0.5 % or 5 %, and each produces a different amount of cropped screen content at
every aspect.

**Why it blocks** — triage test (2), and §1.8 pre-declares this exact case a gap rather than a
judgement call. The consequence is concrete: at 32:9 a 2 % bleed on a 16:9 screen crops real UI, and
whether that is acceptable is an art-direction call. It is also **invisible until someone looks at
the right aspect**, which is how it survived three by-eye sign-offs today — the three overfill
renders confirm no frame shows, and none of them can show whether 2 % crops too much.

It does **not** block S37, whose done-when ("no frame visible at rest at 1.33, 1.78, 2.33") is met
and verified by eye.

**Options I can see** — **(a)** promote it to a `PlayerSettings` knob in the "Picture wall" group,
`wall_overfill_margin`, default 1.02 — satisfies §1.8 as written, makes it live-tunable in S34's
tool alongside every other wall number, and costs one export · **(b)** put it on `WallLayout`
instead, so it ships with the layout rather than with player settings — consistent with GAP-008's
ruling that geometry belongs to the layout, though this is framing rather than packing geometry ·
**(c)** leave it a constant and record that overfill is not author-tunable — cheapest, and defensible
only if the exact value is genuinely never going to be argued with · *my recommendation* **(a)**,
because S34's done-when requires *"no knob above requires editing a `.tres` or a `.gd` by hand to
change"*, and under (c) this one would

**Blast radius** — `UI/Wall/wall_picture.gd`; `DESIGN.md` §5's table; S34's tool, which must expose
every "Picture wall" knob. No test asserts the value today, so changing its home is cheap now.

**Meanwhile** — parked: the margin's home and whether 1.02 is the right number. Continued on:
everything else. S37 and S13 are complete and verified by eye at three aspects.
