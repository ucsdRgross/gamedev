implies"*. Its note: *"A plan dealt FROM the deck is the first mechanism that makes a bigger deck

**What the ANSWER says** — `Q75`=(a), the owner's note: *"with suit effects firing only on a matched

default. Neither answer was made against a measurement — the sim could not model marks until S15.

**What it does not say** — what the curve does when the measurement contradicts the premise.
Measured at S15 with GAP-041's exact invocation (`--grid-goals --trials 800 --q 0.25`, par 0.9,
booster spread, seed 42; the unmarked table reproduced digit for digit first), par play's median
show score with the plan dealt and matched:

```
    N  cells  placed   median (marks)   GAP-041 median (no marks)   ratio
   20     25    20.0        44840             9360                  4.8x
   25     25    25.0       308083            60984                  5.05x
   30     25    25.0        76403            18360                  4.16x
   35     25    25.0        49550            13311                  3.72x
   40     25    25.0        36757            11088                  3.31x
```

The plan is a driver — it multiplies par play's score by 3.3x to 5.1x — but it multiplies MOST
where the deck is dense and LEAST where it is wide, so the ladder still peaks at node 3 and ends the
run at **0.10x** of that peak, against GAP-041's 0.16x. Both of GAP-041's causes are untouched by
this design: `placed` is pinned at 25.0 from node 3 on (the 25-cell cap), and booster packs still
dilute the ranks that make melds. The fits against the marked ladder:

```
  LEAST SQUARES: goal(N) = 42726.9 * (N/20)^3.02  R2(log)=0.6263  worst 88% out
  MINIMAX:       goal(N) = 47308.6 * (N/20)^2.97  worst 101% out
  BEATABLE:      goal(N) = 23400.0 * (N/20)^-0.32  tightness 0.53 of the ladder
```

The beatable fit — the rule GAP-041's shipped constants were chosen under — now returns a NEGATIVE
`goal_alpha`: goals that fall as the deck grows, which contradicts `goal_for`'s contract and turns
`Tests/Map/test_run_manager.gd`'s goals-grow-with-boosters check red. Under it the run-win rates are
13.0% / 11.8% / 8.5% (skilled / par / average), near GAP-041's 17.2 / 15.2 / 10.5 band but below it.
Meanwhile the SHIPPED constants (`goal_g0` 5376, `goal_alpha` 0.26) are now trivial: par play
clears every node by 4.4x to 5.7x at the 25th percentile.

**Why it blocks** — the design contradicts the measurement (triage 3): `Q120`=(c) asks this design
to close a gap whose causes it does not touch. Choosing what the curve does instead is a balance
ruling (triage 2), visible to the player at every End Show.

**Options I can see** —
**(a)** **refit `goal_g0` at a FLAT `goal_alpha`** (alpha 0, `goal_g0` around 18720 — the beatable
fit's value at the run's end) so goals are non-trivial again, leaving GAP-041 open for
poker-patience — consequence: every node winnable and no longer trivial; no growth term; the
goals-grow-with-boosters check needs re-reading against a flat curve ·
**(b)** **`Q120`=(b): ship today's constants and refit in a separate balance pass** — consequence:
nothing changes now, and every goal in the game is trivial until that pass ·
**(c)** **close GAP-041 through one of its own options this design cannot supply** — grow the board
with the deck (GAP-041 (a)) or narrow what a booster adds (GAP-041 (b)) — as a poker-patience v3,
then refit — consequence: the only path to a real growth term; new work outside this design's scope,
and the refit waits on it ·
**(d)** **ship the measured negative alpha** (`goal_g0` 23400, `goal_alpha` -0.32) — consequence: the
beatable curve as fitted, and a run whose goals fall per booster, against the formula's contract and
its test ·
*my recommendation* (a) now, because it is one number and keeps the game playable, and (c) as the
real fix.
