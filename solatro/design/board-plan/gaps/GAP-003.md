# GAP-003 — "re-deal a line" has no signature and no semantics
status: resolved
outcome: answered
raised: during S10 (found by the Phase 4 adversarial review)
design: solatro/design/board-plan/DESIGN.md version 1, nodes D11, QR5
severity: GAP
resolution: |
  Owner, verbatim: "i would consider a line to be a single row/col/diag" and "it should be rerolling a
  line since that is what i think is the context? may also need a reroll entire grid board. there
  shouldnt be empty cells."
  Read with the run's recommendation taken (option (a)'s shape, the owner's word): reroll_line(
  section: ScoringSection) re-deals EVERY cell of one row, column or diagonal, covered or not, from
  the fewest-copies pool through Board.deal_marks, and reroll_grid(grid: int) does the same for every
  cell of one grid. There are no empty cells - every cell carries a mark - so nothing is skipped.
  Step S20.

**What the design says** — `QR5`=(c): *"rerollable by effects — the plan is fixed by default but
content may redraw one cell, swap two marks, or re-deal a line"*. Chart D, D11: *"effects may
reroll one cell, swap two marks, or re-deal a line"*. PLAN §1.7 repeats all three.

**What the ANSWER says** — `QR5`'s follow-ups (*"what a reroll is allowed to touch, whether it can
create a mark on an occupied cell, and what the player sees when one changes"*) were never promoted
to questions, and `NAMES.md`'s CardEffectApi block fixes only `reroll_mark`, `grant_mark` and
`swap_marks`; TEST_PLAN TP-52 lists the same three. The third capability was dropped by both
derived documents, so nothing names it and nothing tests it.

**What it does not say** — what "a line" is for an effect (a row, a column, a diagonal, a
`ScoringSection`?), whether every cell of the line is cleared before the re-deal or only the empty
ones, and whether the re-deal draws from the fewest-copies pool like the opening deal. The reroll
pool itself is under-specified: the run's `reroll_mark` excludes the identity it replaces from the
offer (logged in ASSUMPTIONS.md), which is one defensible reading of "redraw".

**Why it blocks** — an API shape content will be written against, with two or more defensible
signatures; expensive to reverse once an effect ships.

**Options I can see** —
**(a)** **`redeal_line(section: ScoringSection)`** — every cell of the section, empty or covered, is
cleared and re-dealt from the fewest-copies pool — consequence: one seam reusing `Board.deal_marks`;
covered cells change under their cards, which the live match already tolerates ·
**(b)** **`redeal_line(coord: BoardCoord, axis)`** — the row or column through a cell, empty cells
only — consequence: a covered cell's mark is untouched; a second argument nobody has named ·
**(c)** **defer** — ship the two writers that exist and add the third when an effect wants it —
consequence: `QR5`=(c) stays partly unimplemented and the docs say so ·
*my recommendation* (c) until content asks; then (a).

**Blast radius** — plan step S10; `NAMES.md` CardEffectApi block; TEST_PLAN TP-52.
**Meanwhile** — parked: the third writer. Continued on: everything else.
