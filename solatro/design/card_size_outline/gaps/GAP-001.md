# GAP-001 — the 40x54 card makes a 7-column board WIDER THAN THE WINDOW

**Status:** OPEN — needs the owner. Parked; nothing else in this feature depends on it.
**Found:** 2026-08-06, by `test_ui_props` → *"every spawned fire entered the visible viewport"*.

## The measurement

Instrumented the live prop watcher during a real submit on the test's crafted board:

```
OFFSCREEN fire at (1187.0, 134.5)   viewport rect [P: (0,0), S: (1152.0, 648.0)]
```

**x = 1187 against a window of 1152.** It is the RIGHT edge, not the bottom — the fire prop on the
rightmost column spawns 35 px past the visible area and never enters it, so the check fails honestly.

## Why it happens, and why it is not a test artifact

A card is `40 x 54` art units at `card_scale = 2.5`, so **100 x 135 screen px where it was 95 x 125**.
That is **+5 px of width per column**. The board in question is 7 columns wide:

```
7 columns x 5 px = 35 px      = exactly the overflow measured
```

⚠ **The board was already sitting EXACTLY on the window edge before this change** (1187 − 35 = 1152).
So the outline did not introduce a margin problem; it consumed the last of a margin that was already
zero. Any future change that widens a card or a gutter by even one unit lands in the same place.

`CARD_SEPARATION` 14 → 16 is *not* a contributor here — it is vertical stacking, and the overflow is
horizontal. The cause is `CARD_SIZE.x` 38 → 40 alone.

## What is NOT wrong

- `PlayArea` sits inside a `SmoothScrollContainer`, so the board is reachable in game — it scrolls.
  Nothing is unreachable or broken.
- The check is not too strict. It exists because the owner reported (2026-07-13) that hoops
  "never show in the real view", and it is the guard that caught that. Relaxing it to go green would
  delete the guard for the exact class of bug it was built for.

## What the owner has to decide

The question is a game-feel one and it is theirs:

1. **Accept it.** Props on the rightmost column are momentarily off-screen unless the player has
   scrolled. Then the TEST needs a narrower crafted board (it is asserting prop visibility, not board
   fit), and that change should say in one line that it is dodging a known overflow, not fixing one.
2. **Drop the default `card_scale`** from 2.5 (`player_settings.gd:21`). `2.375` puts a 7-column board
   back inside 1152 px with the old margin. Everything on the board is derived from `card_scale`, so
   this is a one-line change — but it makes every card smaller, which is the opposite of the point of
   growing them.
3. **Tighten `PlayArea.separation`** (currently 4, `play_area.gd:21`). Dropping it to 3 recovers ~6 px
   per gutter, more than paying for the card's +5 — at the cost of columns reading as more crowded,
   right when each card just gained a hard outline that makes its edge more assertive.

⚠ **Whichever wins, the zero-margin condition is the real finding** and is worth a guard of its own:
nothing today asserts that a full-width board fits the window, which is why this surfaced as a fire
prop being invisible rather than as a layout check.

## Until it is answered

`test_ui_props`'s *"every spawned fire entered the visible viewport"* is **left FAILING on purpose.**
It is reporting a true fact about the board. Making it pass without a decision would mean either
weakening the guard or quietly picking one of the three options above on the owner's behalf.
