## Place Your Bets!
Once you beat the Hearts deck for the first time or you use the unlock all setting in the menu, you will see the screen to place your bet once you have selected your starting deck. Selecting your chosen bet will change the game play rules based off your selection as shown in the table below:
| Bet Type | Game Rule Changes
| Play it Safe | (None) Standard Rules
| Double Down! | Advantage is harder to get.
| Triple Down! | Start each encounter with a Minor Arcana 5 in your draw pile. Advantage is harder to get.
| Quadruple Down! | Foes have better decks! Start each encounter with a Minor Arcana 5 in your draw pile. Advantage is harder to get.
| ALL IN! | Face the Final Boss. You hit first. Foes have better decks! Start each encounter with a Minor Arcana 5 in your draw pile. Advantage is harder to get.

# Card Suits
In real-life blackjack, card suits do not matter for gameplay. However, in Dungeons and Degenerate Gamblers, the suit of a card can have a profound effect on either character's health, chips and shield. 
Suit Effects
| Suit | Effect | Encounter Effects | Icon
| Hearts | Heal HP | On blackjack, recovers HP.
| Clubs | Deal Extra Damage | On blackjack, increase damage dealt.
| Spades | Gain Shields | On blackjack, gain shields, but only applies to current encounter.
| Diamonds | Gain Chips | On blackjack, gain extra chips.
| Nothings | No Effect | Cards of this suit provide no additional, or blackjack, effect.
| All Suits | Hearts/Clubs/Spades/Diamonds | Combines the effects of all card suits on blackjack.
## Suit Effects
Suit effects trigger only if one of the players wins a round with their max score; on a max-score tie, neither player gets these. 
On a winning blackjack, all suit effects happen in proportion to how many of each suit went into the blackjack, but only cards that have positive values will count. For instance, having a negative-valued heart card in play will not cause the player to lose HP when winning a round with blackjack.
Some examples:
Suppose you win a round with a 4 of Clubs, 8 of Diamonds, 3 of Spades and 6 of Hearts (totalling 21), while your opponent stood on 15. You will earn 8 chips, gain 3 shield, heal 6 HP, and deal 4 extra damage on top of the normal score difference. In this case, that works out to (21 - 15) + 4 = 10 damage.
Suppose you win a round with an Ace of Nothings and a Jack of All Trades. The 11 points from the Ace do not give you any effects, but the Jack means you heal 10 HP, earn 10 chips, gain 10 shield and deal 10 extra damage.
Cards can have negative values, and these decrease the total of their suit accordingly; however, no suit's effect will ever be reduced below 0, even if its total is. Thus, for example, playing a Negative 4 of Diamonds and some combination of Spade cards totaling 25 is a blackjack (-4 + 25 = 21) that grants you 25 shield.
Additionally, a negative-valued card with all suits will subtract from every suit's effect. For example, playing four 7s of all main suits and a negative 7 with all suits will total to 21, but it will give no blackjack bonuses, since the negative 7 reduces all suits' effects to 0. However, no cards naturally have a negative value and all suits.

# Cards
This page details all the cards that exist in Dungeons & Degenerate Gamblers.
Each card has a value (always an integer) and a suit (Hearts, Diamonds, Spades, Clubs, Nothings, or All Suits At Once). Most cards also have other properties that activate under certain conditions:
On play: Activates immediately when the card is played.
On stand: Activates when the player whose side the card is on has stood.
On tie: Activates if both players have stood and their scores are equal.
On discard: Activates when this card is discarded (except via a "quick discard" effect).
On taking damage: Activates when player whose side the card is on loses health
Other effects such as Exploit: An effect that the player can trigger manually by clicking on the card. Costs 1 Advantage.
## Numbered Cards
These are cards that, by and large, have no extra properties beyond their suit and value. Aces and numbers 2-10 behave exactly as standard playing cards do in real-life Blackjack. Besides these, Dungeons & Degenerate Gamblers has standard-suited cards of the following values:
| Name | Base value | Extra effect
| 1 | 1 | Unlike Aces, these cards never take value 11 by themselves.
| 11 | 11 | Unlike Aces, these cards never take value 1 by themselves.
| 12 | 12
| 13 | 13
| 21 | 21
| 0 | 0
| Negative Ace | -1 | Flexible (-1 or -11).
| Negative n | -n | Available in values from -1 to -11 in each suit.
| Pi | 3 | On play: Set value to 3. On stand: rounds up.
| Half | 0 | On play: Set value to 0. On stand: rounds up.
There are also numbered Nothings cards, but these are only available in numbers 1-11 and Aces.
All Negative cards' art is the same as their positive counterparts, but with the colors flipped (white numbers and pips on a red or black background).
The Counterfeiter in Jack's Tavern can modify all numbered cards except Aces, Negative Aces, Pi, Half, and 21. The following special cases occur when certain choices would make an illegal card:
Decreasing a 1 of Nothings produces a Blank Card.
Modifying a Blank Card in either direction produces a Glitched Card.
Increasing a 13 of any suit, or an 11 of Nothings, produces a Glitched Card.
Decreasing a Negative 11 of any suit produces a Glitched Card.
Note that Glitched Cards are not eligible for the Counterfeiter.

# Effects
In Dungeons & Degenerate Gamblers, a vast majority of cards have conditionals that manipulate gameplay in various ways. These conditionals are called "effects". Listed below is a list of all the card effects in Dungeons & Degenerate Gamblers:
| Name | In-game description | Explanation
| Blackjack | Score is equal to bust limit. | If a player's current score is exactly equal to their maximum score, Blackjack is achieved.
| Burn | Card is removed for this encounter. | Burned cards automatically return to the player's deck between encounters.
| Bust Limit | The Maximum score (usually 21). | The bust limit may be manipulated by the effects of other cards, such as the Rules Card which can increase or decrease the player or opponent's bust limit by 1.
| Create | Put in play a card that lasts for this encounter only. | Cards created with this effect are not considered a true part of your deck and are immediately removed after the encounter is beaten.
| Exploit | Spend 1 advantage to enact this effect. | Exploiting a card causes the opponent to hit or stand afterwards, which can be useful for seeing them play out the round before hitting yourself.
| Flexible | Takes the highest value that does not cause a bust. | Flexible cards have two values and take their higher value (in all zones) as long as the sum of that value and the player's current score does not exceed the bust limit. The archetypal Flexible card, the ace, has a value of 11 unless the player's score is 11 or higher, which causes it to use its lower value of 1 instead (assuming the standard bust limit of 21).
| Foil | Handy at the start of encounter, and free to play from hand. | At the start of the encounter, all Foil cards from the player's deck are immediately added to the hand. Playing a Foil card from hand never costs advantage (including if the card was added back to the hand later in the encounter). If an opponent has Foil cards in their deck, they are all played immediately at the beginning of the encounter.
| Foresight | View certain number of cards from draw pile in draw order. | "Foresight X" reveals the next X cards that will be drawn from the deck on hit. This may change if more cards are shuffled into the draw pile (such as via the Yellow Card).
| Handy | Moves to hand. | Handy cards cannot be played from the deck (moving directly into the player's hand instead). The player's hand can contain arbitrarily many cards, which can be played onto the table at any time at a cost of 1 Advantage per card, unless the card is Foil. Note that foes don't have hands of their own; any effects that would move cards into their hands or create cards in their hands (such as Booster Pack) cause them to play all those cards immediately.
| Instant Blackjack | Set this card's value to whatever gives blackjack. | For example, if this effect triggers while the player's score is 10 and their bust limit is 21, the card's value is set to 11. The value of the card in question does not change when additional cards are hit, played from hand, or otherwise appear on the player's side of the table. Note that the card's value will stay the same even after being discarded and played again (except Loyalty Card).
| Instant Tie | Set this card's value to whatever means scores will be equal. | Similar to Instant Blackjack, but the card's value is set so that the player who played the card has an equal score as their opponent. For example, if you are at 19, your opponent is at 13, a card with Instant Tie will be set to -6. Like Instant Blackjack, the value is not affected by subsequent cards played.
| Lock | Card cannot be discarded. | Locked cards are not discarded between rounds and cannot be discarded by effects that discard or quick discard played cards, but they can still be burned.
| Quick Damage | Damage that does not trigger "on taking damage" effects.
| Quick Discard | Discard without enacting "on discard" effects.
| Rounds Up | +1 value if this does not bust. | If multiple cards on the field would Round Up simultaneously, they Round Up in the order that they were played.
| Shred | Permanently remove from deck and this encounter.
| Wish | Has a chance to bring the wished for card to the top of your draw pile. | Wish for a card in your deck If it's the top of your deck it's 100% to succeed half way into your deck is a 50% to succeed there's a minimum 33% success rate for any card in your deck

# Encounters
(This section is still being worked on. Pardon our dust.) 
An encounter in D&DG consists of playing blackjack against an opponent who has a deck unique to them. An encounter has two possible end states: success and failure. Once an encounter with an opponent is initiated, the player cannot disengage. When a player chooses to "Save and Exit", the encounter restarts from the state it was initiated with and all progress done during the encounter is lost. If an encounter is beaten, whereas the player successfully brings the opponent to zero health or less, the player progresses, else, the entire run is considered as failed and the player will have to restart.

# Events
An event in D&DG is one of the available options the player can select in a run that is not considered to be an encounter. Strategically, if a player is not fully confident in either their deck or their current health, an event can provide the possibility of boosts to a player's stats, or the manipulation of one's own deck. Each floor has their own unique events, with only a small number of events being repeated.
## Shops
Shops are able to appear on any floor.
Each shop can only be used once and you can only use one per floor.

The roulette wheel can be spun multiple times, with quadratically increasing chip costs each time. Specifically:
The first spin costs 21 chips.
Subsequent spins have increasing costs, with the increases themselves increasing linearly:
The 2nd spin costs 21 more than the 1st spin.
The 3rd spin costs 42 more than the 2nd spin.
The 4th spin costs 63 more than the 3rd spin.
In general, the n'th spin will cost 21(n-1) chips more than the (n-1)st spin.
The formula for the cost of the n'th spin is C(n) = 21(1 + n(n-1)/2). The formula for the combined cost of the first n spins (i.e. how much you need to have in order to afford n spins of the wheel) is S(n) = 7n(n²+5)/2.

## Synergies
## Relics
Broken Chip, as reducing your opponent's max score to 20 can make them bust easier.
Fish and Chips, as increasing your max score not only allows you to have more cards in play, but can also guarantee winning blackjacks if your opponent's max score is lower than yours.
Chip on Your Shoulder becomes easier to use with a higher max score.
## Cards
+1 max score means +1 blackjack effect, which is universally good, but also rather nice for Treasure Vault.
If you increase your max score, all cards that trigger on hitting at a certain score: Heart of Threes, Club of Fours, Spade of Fives, and Diamond of Sixes, become easier to use.
