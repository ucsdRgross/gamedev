# GAP-012 — the map has no pad route into the description panel

status: ruled -- building
owner ruling: takes the recommendation, option (a): on the map, `ui_up` at the top of ANY shown description moves the focus into the panel, onto the X. The owner's words: "take recommendations for 11 and 12 gap". The map binds `ui_up` and `ui_left` to the same backward node cycle, so `ui_left` keeps cycling and nothing is lost.
raised: at the close of the gap run, by the adversarial review and the code review of `GAP-010`=c
design: DESIGN.md version 10, nodes K9, K10; `Q137`=a; `GAP-010`=c
severity: OWNER CALL (input), and it decides whether `GAP-010`=c's pad half is reachable

**What the ruling says** — `GAP-010`=(c) was chosen because *"it is the only way back a pad and a
touch player can find without the pointer"*, and *"every option except (d) also needs the preview
cards made reachable by mouse, pad and touch (`Q137`=a, the multi-modal rule)"*. `Q137`=(a): *"yes —
the same rule as the board; whatever is selected is described"*.

**What the code does** — the listed cards take pad focus, a focus pick lands on `%Back`, and the
way back rests the focus on the picked card (`TestSidebar` 1.17, 1.19). But nothing carries a pad
from the map's picture INTO the panel: on the board, `HudContainer._navigates_to_exit` moves the
focus onto the X on `ui_up` at the top of a LOCKED description, and Godot's focus search never
crosses a viewport. The map never locks — an accept on a node ENTERS it (K4) — so `is_locked()` is
never true there and no press reaches the panel. A pad player on the map sees the pack described
and cannot reach its cards or the way back. Rows 1.17 and 1.19 start with `grab_focus()` on a
listed card, which is the pick, not the route to it.

**What the design does not say** — how a pad enters the sidebar on a screen that has no lock.

**Options I can see** — **(a)** the board's rule without the lock: on the map, `ui_up` at the top of
ANY shown description moves the focus into the panel (onto the X, from which the neighbour search
reaches the listed cards) · **(b)** a dedicated action, `sidebar_focus`, bound to a pad button and a
key, that moves the focus into the panel from any screen and back out with cancel · **(c)** the map
locks too — a second accept on the described node locks its description the way a board click
does, and the board's route applies unchanged; travel then needs a distinct press · *my
recommendation* (a): it is the board's rule minus the precondition the map cannot meet, adds no
action and no new press, and the X already anchors the panel's focus chain

**Blast radius** — (a): `UI/hud_container.gd` `_navigates_to_exit` (the `is_locked()` term becomes
screen-aware), one `TestSidebar` row that walks by real keys from a map node into a listed card and
back · (b): `project.godot` InputMap, `hud_container.gd`, the settings screen · (c): `Levels/map.gd`,
`WorldMapController`, and the travel press
**Meanwhile** — a pad player reads the pack and cannot open its cards; the mouse and a finger can.
