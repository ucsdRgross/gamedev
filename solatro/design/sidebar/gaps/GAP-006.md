# GAP-006 — a pad/keyboard player's armed card follows a mouse position nobody is controlling
status: built
raised: at the Phase 5 boundary review (S14–S18)
design: DESIGN.md §1.4, nodes G5–G9; answers Q249, Q261, Q262, Q265
severity: OWNER CALL (a contradiction inside the design; not blocking)
owner ruling: takes the recommendation, option (b) - "a key/pad focus does not start following: the armed card stays lifted in its slot and only a mouse motion or a touch starts it". The owner's words: "i will
take recommendation for all gaps". Built, one commit per gap, on branch `sidebar`.

**What the design says** — PLAN §1.4, literally: a following card targets the pointer
(`get_global_mouse_position()`). `Q262`=(a): following starts on "focus landing on any card by key
or pad, OR any mouse motion at all". `Q249`=(d), the owner's words: *"the selection glow is over
where selector is to show current selection as normal"* — the SELECTOR is what a key/pad player
moves. TEST_PLAN 9.4 expects an armed card "lifted, glowing, focus elsewhere" after a key focus.

**What the code does** — `PlayArea.on_control_focus_entered` calls `follow_cards()` on any
non-resting focus, and `CardVisual` then eases toward `get_global_mouse_position()`. In the
picture's SubViewport that is the last pushed pointer position: nothing, or wherever the mouse was
parked. Fresh show, no mouse input, press Right on the pad: the lifted card leaves its slot and
glides to a point the player never chose. `armed_focus_elsewhere.png` shows this state (the card
at the viewport centre, `following=true`). 9.4's state is unreachable by any input.

**Options I can see** — **(a)** the follow target is the FOCUSED CONTROL when following was
started by a key/pad focus, and the pointer when it was started by mouse motion; a later event of
the other kind switches the target · **(b)** a key/pad focus does not start following at all — the
armed card stays lifted in its slot and only a mouse motion (or a touch) starts it; the pad player
places by accept on a cell, which already works · **(c)** as built: the pointer is the only target
· *my recommendation* (b), because it is the smaller change, makes 9.4 reachable, and matches
`Q249`'s "glow is over where selector is" — the selector moves, the card waits
**Blast radius** — `UI/play_area.gd` (`on_control_focus_entered`, `follow_cards`),
`Cards/card_visual.gd`'s follow target under (a); TEST_PLAN 9.4 and §6; DESIGN §1.4 text
**Meanwhile** — as built; a pad-only player sees the card fly to the last pointer position
