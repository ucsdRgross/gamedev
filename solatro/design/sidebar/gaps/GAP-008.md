# GAP-008 — a mouse click-lock on any grabbable card is dismissed by the motion a placement needs
status: built
raised: at the Phase 5 boundary review (S14–S18); first noted in the S14 task entry
design: DESIGN.md §1.2 B9, §1.4; answers Q56, Q62, Q267, Q268
severity: OWNER CALL (the design's own note warned of this outcome; not blocking)
owner ruling: takes the recommendation, option (a) - "the cell-leave dismissal applies only to a card the player did NOT click to lock". The owner's words: "i will
take recommendation for all gaps". Built, one commit per gap, on branch `sidebar`.

**What the design says** — `Q56`=(a): a click on a card "grabs or places AND locks the
description". `Q62`=(b): the lock closes on "the cursor leaving the bounds of the cell the card
came from, while the card is held". `Q267`=(a): a clicked card "follows the cursor immediately".
`Q268`=(a): the cell-leave trigger "only arms once the card is FOLLOWING". `Q62`'s own note on
option (a): "makes click-to-lock and click-to-grab cancel each other out on any grabbable card,
which is probably not what you want".

**What the code does** — the four rulings read together, literally. Click Entrance card B: B's
description locks, B is grabbed and follows at once, and the pointer is inside B's cell. Move toward
any cell: the lock dies the moment the pointer crosses B's cell edge — before any placement is
possible. On the mouse a lock on a grabbable card is indistinguishable from a hover. On touch the
same journey keeps the lock until the placement, because no motion arrives between taps. S14 built
this reading and flagged it ("OWNER SHOULD SEE"); the review confirms the journey is the one
`Q62`'s note said not to want.

**Options I can see** — **(a)** the cell-leave dismissal applies only to a card the player did NOT
click to lock — a click-locked card keeps its description through the drag and the placement
closes it (B11 already says "place closes") · **(b)** a click on a grabbable card locks without
grabbing when the card is already armed, and grabs without locking otherwise — the two verbs never
share a click · **(c)** as built · *my recommendation* (a) — one rule change at the dismissal
site, and the touch journey already behaves this way
