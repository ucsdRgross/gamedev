## OWNER ANSWER (by standing authorisation) — (a), the camera zooms OUT in the overview

**The overview zooms the CAMERA out to frame the whole picture — every grid position at once.
Focusing zooms it back in to one position.** That restores `H5` and makes the two modes mean
different things again.

⚠ **AND IT IS BLUR-SAFE, which is why it survives `GAP-024`'s objection.** `GAP-024` rejected moving
the FOCUSED zoom to the camera because that MAGNIFIES a fixed `3656x685` texture by `1.94` and
visibly softens the card art. **Zooming OUT MINIFIES** — the opposite direction — so the overview
loses no sharpness. The focused mode keeps the scroller's crisp re-render exactly as `GAP-024`=(b)
ruled. The two answers compose.

## What was found — MEASURED

After `GAP-024`=(b) the camera's zoom is the same in both modes; only `board_zoom` (the scroller's
content scale) differs, `1.0` overview vs `1.940559` focused. So both modes frame the same ~1194 px
of picture. With `S31e` spreading grids one per `1219` px position (cell blocks at `504 / 1723 /
2942`), **a neighbouring grid is never inside the camera's rect.**

Three checks report it, each from a different angle:
```
TP-140 precondition: in the OVERVIEW a neighbouring grid is in frame beside the middle one
                     -- grid 0 false grid 2 false
TP-106: the grid the view rests on is wholly in frame before panning -- [0] in frame, resting on 1
TP-101 precondition: three grids are wider than the window -- content 3640.000000 window 3656.000000
```
⚠ **`TP-140`'s precondition is the instrument-proving one** — it exists to show the test can SEE a
neighbour before asserting that focusing removes it. It is not calibration noise; it is the check
telling us the overview has stopped being an overview.

## Why this is a gap and not a defect

`H5` (*"as many grids as fit at a readable zoom"*) and `H22` (*"the picture frame holds 3 grid
positions; the camera steps between them"*) were both satisfiable while grids were packed adjacent
INSIDE one position. `GAP-021` spread them one per position, and `GAP-024`=(b) made the camera the
overview's view. Neither ruling noticed it left `H5` unsatisfiable. **No answered node says what the
overview's zoom should be**, and two defensible answers differ in observable behaviour.

## Options

- **(a) TAKEN — the camera zooms out in the overview.** Frames the whole picture, all grid positions
  visible; focusing zooms back to one. Restores `H5` and `H6` (*"all-grids is orientation: clicking
  a grid zooms in on it"*), which is meaningless if only one grid is ever shown. Minification, so no
  blur. ⚠ Costs: `H22`'s STEPPING becomes a focused-mode motion rather than an overview one, since a
  fully-zoomed-out overview has nothing to step across — `TP-105` must be re-checked against that.
- **(b) Accept a one-grid overview.** The modes differ only in content scale. Cheapest — nothing to
  build. Costs `H5`, `H6` and the point of having two modes at all.
- **(c) Re-pack the grids adjacent again**, so several fit inside one camera window. Undoes
  `GAP-021`, which the owner ruled deliberately. Rejected.
