# GAP-016 — grid-stepping rides the scroller, but `QR3`=a and `H11`/`H23` put it on the camera

status: open
outcome: —
raised: during execution plan step S27 (Back/Forward zoom, pan actions, snap); blocks S28
design: DESIGN.md §36 flowchart H (H11, H13, H22, H23), `QR3`, `Q159`, `Q182`; PLAN.md §3
severity: CONTRADICTION

## What the design says

`QR3` = **(a)**, verbatim from the option the owner chose:

> *"the wall's camera pans over a wide picture — the game picture becomes several times wider than
> the window, and the wall camera's resting pose on it becomes a family of poses instead of one"*

Flowchart H divides the two mechanisms explicitly, and `DESIGN.md` §36 spells the division out in
prose:

> *"The **wall camera** steps between the three grid positions the picture frame holds. The **one
> scroll container** handles everything else — tall stacks, and revealing more of a single oversized
> grid. They never contend, because they move different things."*

- **H11** "Camera2D limit and smoothing do the clamping"
- **H22** "picture frame holds 3 grid positions; the camera steps between them"
- **H23** "camera pans between grid positions; the scroller reveals more of ONE grid"
- **Q159** = (b), which inherits (a)'s named mechanism: clamped *"like `Wall.clamp_pan` does for the
  wall, and using `Camera2D.limit_*` … rather than hand-rolled arithmetic"*

## What shipped, and why

`S27` implemented the grid-stepping pan on the existing `SmoothScrollContainer`, using its own
`scroll_x_to` clamp and overdrag for the bound and the bounce. **So the scroller is currently doing
the camera's job**, which is the one division `H23` says never happens.

The practical defence is real and should not be dismissed: the play area contains **no `Camera2D`**
— the board is a `SmoothScrollContainer` — and the wide picture that a camera would pan across is
built by **`S31`** (`H1`–`H3`: `design_size` sized for 3 grids, the height rule, the render-target
clamp), which is in **Phase 7, after this phase**. `S27` could not have panned a wide picture that
does not exist yet.

⚠ **The citation offered for it does not hold, and that is what makes this a gap rather than a
judgement call.** `Q182` — *"the existing `SmoothScrollContainer`, driven programmatically"* — was
cited as the ruling that outranks `H11`. But `Q182` is gated **`[QR3=b]`**, and `QR3` = **(a)**. It
sits on a pruned branch and **is unanswered**. There is no owner ruling for the scroller.

## Options

- **(a)** — **Reorder: `S31` before `S28`.** Build the wide picture first, then `S28` migrates
  grid-stepping onto the wall camera and the scroller keeps only tall stacks and oversized-grid
  reveal, exactly as `H23` divides them. `S27`'s pan becomes the scroller's half and its
  grid-stepping is replaced. Costs a phase reorder and rewrites `TP-101`/`TP-102`'s mechanism;
  matches the design as written.
- **(b)** — **The scroller keeps grid-stepping, and `H11`/`H23` are amended.** `Q182`'s pruned text
  becomes the real answer, promoted onto the `QR3`=a branch. Cheapest, keeps every `S27` test as
  written, and means the wall camera never moves during a show — which has to be checked against
  `H18`/`H19` (the saved pan) and `H21` (Info mode), all Phase 7.
- **(c)** — **Split by view mode**, which is `QR3`'s own option (c): the wall camera drives the
  all-grids overview, the in-screen scroller drives single-grid panning. `QR3` was answered (a), so
  this is a re-open of that fork, not a new idea.
- **(d)** — **Phase 6 finishes on the scroller and the migration lands in Phase 7 with the picture.**
  `S28` ships `TP-104` (one scroll container only) and `TP-106` (>3 grids shifts which 3 are in
  frame) now, and **`TP-105` — "the camera steps between the 3 grid positions the frame holds" —
  moves to Phase 7 beside `S31`**, since it cannot be satisfied before the wide picture exists.
  No design node is amended and nothing is thrown away; only `TEST_PLAN.md`'s step column changes
  for one row.

## Why this is a gap and not a defect

Two of these differ in observable behaviour — whether the wall's camera moves during a show at all —
and that reaches `H18`/`H19`'s saved pan and `H21`'s Info mode in Phase 7. Reversal is expensive
once `S29`'s cross-grid selection is built on whichever mechanism wins. **A fourth option was looked
for and found — (d)** — per `GAP-014`'s lesson, and it may well be the answer; it is still the
owner's call, because it re-dates one `TEST_PLAN.md` row.

## Blast radius

`S28` (H13, H22, H23, H24 — the step this blocks), `S29` (arrow keys cross a grid boundary "and the
camera follows"), `S30` (re-centring), and Phase 7's `S31`–`S33`. `S26` and `S27` stand either way:
the view MODE and the level stack are mechanism-independent.
