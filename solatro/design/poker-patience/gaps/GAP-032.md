# GAP-032 — Entrance cards do not scale with grid cards, because the Entrance is pinned OUTSIDE the scroll

status: resolved
outcome: answered by the owner — **the Entrance and the grid scale TOGETHER; scaling either scales both**
raised: owner instruction while the 3-grid canvas work was in flight
design: DESIGN.md §36 flowchart H (`H4`, `H6`, `H13`), §34 flowchart F (the Entrance), `GAP-031`
severity: TWO THINGS THAT MUST AGREE ARE DRIVEN BY DIFFERENT QUANTITIES

## OWNER RULING

> *"scale of entrance cards and grid cards should scale together if you are scaling either"*

Whichever scale changes — the focused/overview `board_zoom`, or `card_scale` — **the Entrance row and
the grid cells move together.** They are never at different scales.

## What was measured

```
UI/play_area.gd:545   scroll_container.scale = Vector2.ONE * board_zoom      <- grid cards scale
UI/play_area.gd:1674  `EntranceCardLayer`, pinned outside the board's scroll (`_bind_slot`)
UI/play_area.gd:171   strip_h := CardVisual.CARD_SIZE.y * card_scale * entrance_visible_rows
```

**The grid's cards live inside `scroll_container` and are scaled by `board_zoom`. The Entrance's
cards are on a PINNED layer outside that container, so `board_zoom` never reaches them**, and the
Entrance strip's own height is computed from `card_scale` alone. So entering or leaving the focused
view rescales the board and leaves the Entrance at its old size.

⚠ **The pinning is deliberate and must not simply be undone.** `EntranceCardLayer` exists so the
Entrance's cards *"can never"* be clipped or scrolled away by the board's scroller — that is the
pinned-Entrance behaviour `S20` shipped. The fix has to make the two scales agree **without**
putting the Entrance back inside the scroller.

⚠ **This is the standing rule "EVERY SITE THAT ADDS A MEASURED GLOBAL TO A LOCAL SIZE MUST BE
ZOOM-AWARE" in a new place** — height labels were 47 px out in focused mode for exactly this shape of
reason. Anything that mixes an Entrance quantity with a board quantity must take one into the other's
space explicitly.

## ⚠ Why this matters more now than it did

`GAP-031` gives the OVERVIEW and the FOCUSED view **different zooms by design** (the two modes now
differ by mechanism, not only by content scale), so the board is rescaled more often and by larger
factors than before. A desync that was occasional becomes routine.

## What a fix must satisfy

- One scale drives both. Deriving the Entrance's scale from the board's is preferable to setting the
  same number in two places — **two writers of one observable is how a test ends up measuring which
  ran second.**
- The Entrance stays pinned: never clipped, never scrolled away.
- The strip height (`entrance_visible_rows` x card height) tracks the same scale.
- ⚠ Watch the known open bug: *"for ~11 frames after a view change, a click on an Entrance card
  silently does nothing — the card is easing out from under the cursor."* A scale change on the
  Entrance may lengthen or worsen that window. **Check it rather than assuming.**

## Blast radius

`UI/play_area.gd` (`_apply_board_zoom_rect`, `_bind_slot`, the `EntranceCardLayer` pinning, the strip
height sites at :171 and :199, `_zoom_board_to`), the Entrance's hit-testing, and any check that
asserts an Entrance card's size or position.
