## OWNER ANSWER (by standing authorisation) — (a), the bounce follows the pan onto the camera

`H10` says *"edge of the board bounces"* and `H9` says the pan *"steps one grid at a time, always
landing centred."* Under `GAP-024`=(b) the OVERVIEW's pan is the camera, so **the overview's bounce
must be the camera's too.** One mechanism per mode, matching where the pan already lives.

## What was found

`_bounce_board()` (`UI/play_area.gd:764`) spends a velocity kick into the `SmoothScrollContainer`.
`GAP-024`=(b) moved the OVERVIEW's horizontal pan to the camera and left this untouched, so:

**Measured — the overview has no edge bounce at all.** The scroller it nudges has ~16 px of range
under `GAP-022`=(a), and two GRID VIEW checks report it honestly:
```
the board comes back to its edge: a bounce, not a scroll -- rest 0.000000 -> -97.677208
the last grid is wholly on screen again once the bounce settles -- 1432.322754 px off screen
```
⚠ **The implementer declined to invent a mechanism and flagged it instead** — the right call, because
`grid_bounce_velocity_px` is a SCROLLER-SHAPED number (a velocity fed to a smooth scroller) and
reinterpreting it as camera overdrag is a design decision, not a port.

⚠ **`H10` is a FEEL rule with no test guarding the feel** — only these two checks, which measure
position. It is exactly the kind of thing that gets silently dropped in a migration because nothing
forces anyone to notice its absence.

## Options

- **(a) TAKEN — re-point the bounce onto the camera.** On a step past the end, the camera tweens
  past the limit and settles back, the same shape the scroller's kick produced. Preserves `H10`'s
  feel and keeps ONE mechanism per mode. ⚠ Costs a re-derivation of `grid_bounce_velocity_px` into
  camera-space units, and that conversion must be **stated on the record** rather than tuned by eye
  until it looks right — an unexplained constant here is a tunable literal by another name.
- **(b) Drop the bounce in the overview; the camera simply stops at the last grid.** Cheapest, and
  the current measured behaviour already does this. Costs `H10`, which the design states plainly.
- **(c) Leave `_bounce_board()` on the scroller.** Status quo — the overview silently has no bounce
  while the code still looks as though it does. **Not defensible**; recorded only to be rejected.

## Blast radius

`UI/play_area.gd` (`_bounce_board`, and the overview pan path), `Levels/main.gd`
(`_on_overview_pan_requested`), the two GRID VIEW bounce checks, and `grid_bounce_velocity_px`'s
meaning in `NAMES.md`.

## Meanwhile

⚠ **Do not make the two bounce checks green while the feature is missing.** A green check over a
missing feature is worse than the honest red — and this stream has already shipped one dead feature
whose tests passed.
