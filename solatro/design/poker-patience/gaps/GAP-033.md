# GAP-033 — Entrance cards fan DOWNWARD while every other card stacks upward

status: resolved
outcome: answered by the owner — **the Entrance stacks upward too**; its cards are bottom-anchored
  like grid cards
raised: owner instruction while the 3-grid canvas work was in flight
design: DESIGN.md §34 flowchart F (the Entrance), §33 flowchart E (height and the row shift), `S21`
severity: ONE CARD TYPE CONTRADICTS THE RULE EVERY OTHER CARD FOLLOWS

## OWNER RULING

> *"entrance cards should also stack upwards too since their pips are on bottom of cards like all
> other cards"*

**The pip position is the argument.** Pips sit at the BOTTOM of a card, so a stack must grow upward
for the covered cards' pips to stay readable. That is why grid stacks grow upward, and the Entrance
is subject to the same reason.

## What was measured

`UI/play_area.gd` (`_bind_slot`) states the split outright, and marks it as deferred:

```gdscript
# A GRID card hangs from its control's BOTTOM edge (stacks grow upward, a row shares one bottom
# edge); the Entrance still fans downward from its control tops. The layer the control belongs
# to is exactly that distinction, so it is the one thing asked.
var bottom := target_layer == card_layer
vis.bottom_anchored = bottom
```

So `bottom_anchored` is TRUE for the grid layer and FALSE for `EntranceCardLayer`. **The word
"still" is the tell: the downward fan was known and left alone, not chosen.**

⚠ **The one-line reading — `bottom := true` — is NOT the whole change**, and taking it as such is how
this ships broken:

- **The strip height is measured from the top.** `entrance_visible_rows` (default `1.5`) drives
  `strip_h := CardVisual.CARD_SIZE.y * card_scale * entrance_visible_rows` at `:172`/`:198`, and
  `:566` records that the configured value is the FLOOR of a strip the board may grow past. Flipping
  the anchor changes which edge the fan grows from, so **what `entrance_visible_rows` means has to be
  restated, not just kept.**
- **Hit-testing follows the visual.** ⚠ Known open bug: *"for ~11 frames after a view change, a click
  on an Entrance card silently does nothing — the card is easing out from under the cursor."*
  Reversing the fan direction moves cards through a different path. **Check whether this widens that
  window; do not assume.**
- **`EntranceCardLayer` is pinned outside the board's scroll** so its cards can never be clipped or
  scrolled away. An upward-growing Entrance stack grows toward the board. **Confirm it cannot collide
  with or occlude the bottom row of the grid**, which is the direction the board grows out of.

## ⚠ Pairs with `GAP-032`

`GAP-032` (Entrance and grid cards scale together) and this gap are the same underlying complaint:
**the Entrance was built as a special case and has drifted from the rule every other card follows.**
They touch the same code (`_bind_slot`, the strip height, `EntranceCardLayer`) and should be
implemented as one step with one suite run, not two — but they are recorded separately because they
are separately falsifiable.

## ⚠ IT IS NOT A `bottom_anchored := true` FLIP — measured

`CardVisual.get_card_control_center()` (`Cards/card_visual.gd:662-664`) hangs a bottom-anchored card
from **its own control's bottom edge**. The grid copes with that by **REVERSING CONTROL-BUILD ORDER**:
the topmost/newest card is child 0 and the cell's own zone/frame card is the LAST child
(`_bind_grid_panel:2217-2228`, `update_grid_zone_visuals:2233-2253`).

**The Entrance builds header-first, un-reversed.** A naive flip therefore renders the header card
upside-down, extending off the top of the clipped strip, and misplaces the stack. The real change is:

- mirror the grid's reversed-order convention in `set_card_zone` / `update_card_zone_visuals`;
- rewrite `_entrance_slot_center_global`, whose pure-math formula is documented as fanning *"from
  control tops"*, to measure from a FLOOR the way `_grid_slot_center_global` does.

## OWNER RULING on the highlight block

Reversing the build order invalidates the bespoke selected/held highlight at
`UI/play_area.gd:1773-1810`, whose child indices (`vbox.get_child(0)` / `get_child(1)`) are
Entrance-specific and mirrored nowhere in the grid's parallel code.

> **Owner: port the grid's logic and DROP the bespoke block.** The Entrance uses the grid's
> `on_control_focus_entered` widening, which needs no Entrance-specific branch.

This removes a parallel convention rather than adding one — the same intent as `GAP-032`. ⚠ **The
highlight's APPEARANCE when picking up from the Entrance will change to match the grid's. Verify that
by eye**; do not assume it is unchanged.

## `GAP-032`'s mechanism — measured, and it is well-specified

The Entrance renders at `card_scale` only because `EntranceCardLayer`'s render subtree (via
`%EntranceVScroll`, itself a `ScrollContainer`) carries no `board_zoom` scale, unlike
`SmoothScrollContainer` (`scale = Vector2.ONE * board_zoom`, `UI/play_area.gd:544`).

**Apply the same "scale lives on the ScrollContainer, rect-compensated" pattern
(`_apply_board_zoom_rect`) to `%EntranceVScroll`.** ⚠ This is the standing rule *"THE SCALE MUST LIVE
ON THE SCROLL CONTAINER, NOT ITS CONTENT — a `Container` rewrites its children's scale on every
sort."*

Add ONE shared `entrance_strip_height_px(settings, zoom)` accessor to replace the three inline copies
at `:172`, `:198`, `:523`. The two static picture-sizing sites pass `zoom = 1.0` (the render-target
picture is fixed and zoom-independent); the live strip passes `board_zoom`.
