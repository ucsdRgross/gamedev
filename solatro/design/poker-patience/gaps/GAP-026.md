## OWNER ANSWER (by standing authorisation) — (a), host the real `Main`

The camera-dependent checks get a fixture that instantiates `Levels/main.tscn` and calls
`enter_game()`, reusing the setup already proven in `Tests/Visual/overview_pan_route_probe.gd`.
**The existing bare fixture STAYS** for the focused-mode and scroller checks.

⚠ **The repo's own hard rule decides this**: *"No mocks in tools. A harness hosts the real scene and
the real data; a stand-in cannot disagree with what it models."* A hand-wired `Wall` + `Camera2D`
rig mimicking `Main`'s wiring is exactly such a stand-in — and it would agree with `Main` right up
until it silently did not.

## What was found

`GAP-024`=(b) moved OVERVIEW grid-stepping to the wall camera, so "in frame" now means inside
`WallTransition.visible_rect()`. But **`Tests/UI/test_grid_view.gd`'s fixture (`_stand_up_grids`)
instantiates a bare `GameView` — no `Main`, no `Wall`, no `%Camera2D` anywhere in the suite.**
`PlayArea.overview_pan_requested` is emitted and nothing listens. So there is no live camera to read.

⚠ **This is the same wall a previous `TP-140` re-point hit, and it is why that re-point was
REVERTED**: it reconstructed a rect from `resting_state()`, which knows nothing about grid focus, so
it would have stayed GREEN throughout any camera change regardless of correctness. `GAP-022`=(a)
made that check load-bearing for "out of view"; a green-regardless version of it is worse than the
honest red.

## Options

- **(a) TAKEN — a `Main`-hosted fixture in the same suite.** Real scene, real camera, per hard rule
  6. Costs a heavier fixture for those checks and whatever `Main` drags in.
- **(b) A lighter rig wiring `Wall` + `Camera2D` to `overview_pan_requested` the way `Main` does.**
  Cheaper and faster. **Rejected**: it is a stand-in for `Main`, forbidden by hard rule 6, and the
  failure mode is silent divergence rather than a loud break.
- **(c) Move the camera checks into a suite that already hosts `Main` (`Tests/Wall/`).** Plausible,
  but those suites carry `await_siblings_except` deadlock-ordering constraints with GRID VIEW.
  Rejected as not worth the risk for no gain over (a).
