# GAP-005 — Which bucket does a `HEIGHT_V` line (a vertical stack in one cell) bank into?

status: open
outcome: unanswered
raised: 2026-08-26, during execution plan step S12 (the three buckets and their storage)
design: NAMES.md §2 (`GameData.scores_row`/`scores_col`/`scores_row_h`/`scores_col_h`/`score_special`);
  PLAN.md §1.6, §1.7; DESIGN.md `Q102`, `Q110`
severity: GAP

## What the design says

`NAMES.md` §2 fixes exactly five score containers per grid: `scores_row`, `scores_col`,
`scores_row_h`, `scores_col_h`, `score_special`. `PLAN.md` §1.6 states the three-bucket
model verbatim (`Q110`): row, col, and one special bucket that takes "All diagonal type
scores... future special meld scores will also go to this bucket, regardless of if its
diagonal or not." `DESIGN.md`'s `Q102` addresses a HEIGHT line that runs ALONG a row or
column at a raised level ("a height-line along a row adds to R") — that is `LineKind.ROW`/
`COL` with `height > 0`, already covered by `scores_row_h`/`scores_col_h`.

## What the repo says

`ScoringSection.LineKind` has a fourth member, `HEIGHT_V` — a vertical run of cards stacked
in ONE cell (`DESIGN.md` C8, "a stack of 5 in one cell"), built by `SkillLineDetector` via
`ScoringSection.of_geometric_line`. It is a real, already-scored line kind (S11 landed it),
distinct from both a row/col direction and a diagonal.

## What it does not say

Whether a `HEIGHT_V` line is a "special meld" for `Q110`'s purposes (so it banks into
`score_special` alongside diagonals), or something else the five named containers do not
cover at all.

## Why it matters

`Game.add_line_score`'s new grid-bucket write path (S12) must send every `ScoringSection` it
receives somewhere. Guessing wrong here means a real, already-detected and already-spotlit
line silently banks zero, or banks into a bucket `Q110` never intended it to share.

## What I did

S12 wires the row, col and special buckets, which are unambiguous, and their write path.
`HEIGHT_V` sections currently bank nowhere (the write path recognises `ROW`, `COL` and
`DIAG` only) until this gap is answered — no bucket is guessed at for it.

## Options I can see

- **(a)** `score_special` — a `HEIGHT_V` line is not a row or a column direction, and
  `Q110`'s "regardless of if its diagonal or not" reads as covering exactly this case: any
  meld shape besides row/col shares the one special label.
- **(b)** A new dedicated bucket, since a vertical stack is common enough (every tall build)
  that folding it into "special" alongside rare diagonals may undersell it, and/or clutter
  that one label.
- **(c)** Split evenly or duplicated into `score_row` **and** `score_col`, since a vertical
  stack at one cell simultaneously "belongs" to that cell's row and column members — mirrors
  `Q102`(b)'s reasoning for a horizontal height line, extended to the vertical case.

**Blast radius** — `Game.add_line_score`'s grid-bucket derivation (`Levels/game.gd`); S13's
`grid_score` product, which needs to know how many buckets exist and what feeds each.

**Meanwhile** — parked: `HEIGHT_V`'s bucket. Continued on: the row, col and special buckets
for `ROW`/`COL`/`DIAG` sections, which this gap does not touch.
