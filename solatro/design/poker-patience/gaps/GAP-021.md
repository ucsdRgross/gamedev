status: resolved

## OWNER ANSWER — one grid per position

**Each grid position holds ONE grid, so panning moves between grids rather than across bare
background.** The grids spread across the picture at a pitch of one position width; the camera
steps between them, which is `H22` exactly.

⚠ **THE FURNITURE DOES NOT NEED DUPLICATING, and the design already says so.** The option put to the
owner said each position would carry "its own copy of the board furniture"; that is NOT required:

- **`Q39`** (free text, owner's own words): *"yes, follows the camera, think of it like a player
  hand"* — **the Entrance FOLLOWS THE CAMERA** while uncommitted, and anchors below the committed
  grid after a placement.
- **`Q40` = (a)** — ⚠ **not the default.** Once anchored, *"the Entrance stays with its grid and
  scrolls out of view — you have to come back to place"*.

So there is exactly ONE Entrance. It tracks the view until commit, then stays with its grid.

⚠ **What no answered node fixes is the rest of the HUD** — the Deck, the score labels, the End /
Discard / Rules buttons. Window-pinned furniture that follows the camera is the reading consistent
with `Q39`; duplicating it per position is not. **Report it rather than assuming**, and if it needs
a ruling, raise it.

## What was built, and what it exposed

`S31d` widened `game_picture_design_size` to **3656 x 685** — `grid_max_count` GRID POSITIONS of
1219 x 685 side by side, where a position is the window-shaped rect the wall camera frames at rest.
The camera now has a genuine step: at rest it sees **1217.8 of 3656 px**, leaving **2438 px** of
pan, against **0.6 px** before. `H22`'s premise is true for the first time and the suite is green.

**But the game screen does not reflow into it.** `Levels/game_view.tscn` positions every part of the
screen at AUTHORED ABSOLUTE OFFSETS for a 1152 x 648 window — `PlayContainer` is
`anchors_preset = 0`, `offset_left = 423`, `offset_right = 1142`, and the Deck, score labels and
buttons are likewise fixed offsets. Measured inside the real 3656 px picture, `PlayArea.size` is
still **719 x 636** at x **423 .. 1142**. So:

- the left **1219 px** of the picture holds the whole game, exactly as before;
- the right **2437 px** is **bare background**;
- the camera's RESTING pose is `rect.centre`, i.e. x **1219 .. 2437** — **entirely empty picture.**

Photographed, both view modes, in `Tests/Visual/grid_zoom_shot.tscn`. Everything `S31b`/`S31c` built
is intact inside that left third: the focused grid is still exactly the board window's height

## Why it blocks

`TP-105` (`H22`, the camera stepping) is now buildable in geometry and unbuildable in meaning: the
camera would step from the game onto blank background. `S32`'s `H18`/`TP-115`/`TP-117` save and
restore a pan that has nothing to land on but background, and `S33`'s `H21` frames a
"window-aspect view" of which two of three are empty.

Widening the picture was necessary and is not the error. What is missing is a decision, taken by no
answered node, about **what occupies a grid position other than the focused one.**

## Options

- **(a) The screen STRETCHES to the picture.** `PlayContainer` and the side furniture get anchors
  instead of offsets, so the board window becomes ~2900 px and the side panels sit at the picture's
  far edges. Cheapest structurally. Costs: at rest the camera frames the middle position, so the
  Deck, score column and buttons are OFF SCREEN at rest — the furniture is only reachable by
  panning, which no node asks for.
- **(b) The furniture is per-POSITION; only the BOARD spreads.** Each grid position carries its own
  copy of the screen's side furniture (or the furniture is pinned to the camera rather than the
  picture), and the board's grids are laid out one per position. This is what makes "the camera
  steps between three grid positions" mean "the camera steps between three GRIDS", and it is the
  only option under which `H22` and `H23` describe different motions as §36 says they do. Costs the
  most work, and it makes `Q13`(d)'s centring rule a per-position rule rather than a board rule —
  which needs confirming, because `Q13` is ⚑contract.
- **(c) The picture goes back to one position wide and the camera never steps.** `GAP-019`
  option (d) by another road; the owner declined the equivalent at `GAP-016`.
- **(d) The board spreads across the picture but the furniture stays where it is authored.** The
  picture keeps the game in its left position and grids 1 and 2 extend rightward into the space that
  is currently empty, so panning right reveals grid 2 with no furniture beside it. Matches `Q160`'s
  *"camera will pan over 3 possible grid positions"* most literally and is the smallest change from
  what is built, but leaves the two outer positions without a Deck or a score column.
