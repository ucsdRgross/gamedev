# GAP-040 — the clip that cuts a tall stack is the clip that isolates a neighbouring grid

status: resolved
outcome: answered — (a), with a reason no option carried
raised: acting on the owner's report *"still seeing cards clipping when reaching top buffer. perhaps
solutions is to make it so scroll container doesnt clip contents that go out of its area?"*
design: `DESIGN.md` §36 flowchart H — `H13` (one scroll container, for tall stacks), `H23`,
`Q160`, `Q166`; `TP-140`, `TP-141`; `GAP-039` (isolation, exactly-three and no-clipping are
mutually unsatisfiable)
severity: THE PROPOSED FIX REMOVES A SHIPPED CONTRACT

## The measurement

`CardLayer` — where every `CardVisual` is drawn — sits inside the scroller, and the scroller is the
only thing in the chain that clips:

```
CardLayer < TopLevelVBox < SmoothScrollContainer(clip) < PlayArea
```

Focused, one grid, a three-deep stack, at the shipped defaults:

```
board window   y [88.3 .. 613.2]
cell block     y [42.5 .. 575.6]      top_gap -45.8   <- 45.8 px ABOVE the window, and cut there
top_level_vbox min.y 313.0   actual 350.0   board_edge_pad 54.0
```

**The owner's diagnosis is correct about the mechanism.** `clip_contents` on the scroll container is
what cuts the cards.

⚠ **AND THE SAME CLIP IS THE ISOLATION.** `TP-141` asserts *"grid 0 paints NOTHING outside the board
window while another grid is focused"*, and the two `GRID VIEW` clip-window checks assert the
converse for the focused grid. With `clip_contents` off, a neighbouring grid's cards paint across
the whole picture and `TP-141` fails by construction — it is not a tuning question.

⚠ **THE EDGE PAD IS ALREADY GONE BEFORE ANY CARD IS CUT.** `_give_the_board_a_floor()` reserves
`2 * board_edge_pad_px` by setting `top_level_vbox.custom_minimum_size.y`. A `custom_minimum_size`
is a FLOOR: measured above, the content is 350 against a minimum of 313, so the reservation stopped
applying the moment the board grew past it. Both pads are notional once a stack deepens.

⚠ **THE DESIGN'S OWN ANSWER TO A TALL STACK IS THE SCROLLER**, `Q160` verbatim: *"one scroll
container inside the picture to deal with tall stacks and potentially infinite grids or very large
grids."* A scroller that does not clip is not a scroller. So this is not simply a defect to fix —
the report and the shipped design disagree about what should happen when the board outgrows its
window.

## OWNER ANSWER — (a), and the reason is not tall stacks

> *"turn clip contents off. clipping content cant work because i see it clipping stuff like props
> and animations which specifically go outside edges of board visually."*

⚠ **THE DECIDING FACT WAS NOT IN ANY OPTION ABOVE.** Every option here was argued about a deep
STACK reaching the top of the window. The clip also cuts **props and animations, which are authored
to leave the board's edges on purpose** — so the clip is not a trade-off against tall stacks, it is
breaking effects that were designed to overflow. Under that fact (b), (c) and (d) all fail: they
each keep a clip somewhere the props still have to cross.

This also agrees with the ruling `run_a_card_between_grids_is_never_clipped_away_test` was already
written against — *"out of view"* means OFF CAMERA. **The camera may hide a grid; the clip may not.**

⚠ **ISOLATION IS NOW WHOLLY THE CAMERA'S**, which is what `TP-140` already asserts. `TP-141` asserted
the clip's effect and is retired into a guard that the board does NOT clip; the two clip-window
checks become the stronger statement that nothing is culled anywhere.

## Options

- **(a) Turn `clip_contents` off on the board's scroller, as the report proposes.** Cards are never
  cut; a tall stack draws over the HUD and over whatever is beside it. `TP-141` and the two
  clip-window checks are retired, and grid isolation stops being a rendering property — a focused
  grid's neighbours would have to be hidden some other way, or accepted as visible. Cost: the
  isolation contract `Q166`/`TP-140` is built on, and `GAP-039`'s whole derivation, are about what
  the camera and the window frame; removing the clip changes what "isolated" means.
- **(b) Give the top edge pad REAL content and keep the clip.** A spacer above the grid container,
  inside the scrolled content, so the block never touches the window's top edge at rest and a card
  rising into the buffer has somewhere to go. Cards are still cut once a stack is genuinely taller
  than the window — but that is the scroller doing the job `Q160` gives it, and the player scrolls.
  Cost: does not satisfy the report as written; a deep stack still clips, just later.
- **(c) Clip vertically only where it is needed and horizontally always.** The isolation contract is
  about LEFT and RIGHT neighbours; the reported cutting is at the TOP. Godot's `clip_contents` is
  all-or-nothing on a `Control`, so this means a second node: an outer clipper sized to the board's
  horizontal window and full picture height, with the scroller unclipped inside it. Cards rise
  freely into the picture, neighbours stay hidden. Cost: a new layer in a tree that three separate
  measurements already call fragile, and the vertical overflow then draws over the HUD.
- **(d) Do not let the board outgrow its window at all: re-fit `focused_board_zoom` as stacks
  deepen.** Nothing ever clips because nothing ever overflows. Cost: the whole board shrinks under
  the player's hand every time a stack gets deeper, and `H1` requires the picture stay ONE fixed
  size for a run — the re-fit would be the board's zoom changing mid-show, which is the thing
  `focused_board_zoom()`'s fixed point was written to stop.

## Blast radius

`TP-141`, the two `GRID VIEW` clip-window checks, `TP-140` under (a) and (c); `GAP-039`'s
derivation under (a); `_give_the_board_a_floor()` under (b); `focused_board_zoom()` under (d).
Nothing outside the focused board's own geometry.
