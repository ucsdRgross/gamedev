# GAP-022 — `PlayContainer` was stretched to the whole picture, so no grid can ever leave the board window

status: resolved
outcome: answered — (a) keep the stretch, redefine "out of view" against the CAMERA
raised: while verifying execution plan step `S31e` (one grid per grid position, `GAP-021`'s answer)
design: DESIGN.md §36 flowchart H (`H22`, `H23`), `GAP-021`, `GAP-017` part 3, `Q13`, `Q39`, `Q40`
severity: CONTRADICTION

## OWNER ANSWER — (a) keep the stretch; "out of view" means outside the CAMERA

`PlayContainer` stays picture-wide. **"Out of view" stops meaning "outside the scroll container" and
starts meaning "outside the CAMERA's rect."** The container is not what a player sees through; the
camera is.

⚠ **THIS MAKES THE CAMERA LOAD-BEARING, AND IT IS NOT BUILT YET.** `GAP-016`=(d) parked the camera
migration and `S27` shipped grid-stepping on the `SmoothScrollContainer` instead. Under this answer
the camera is no longer a Phase 7 nicety — **every "out of view" claim on the board now resolves
against it**, so `TP-105`'s camera step stops being a step that follows `S31e` and becomes part of
what makes `S31e` verifiable at all.

**What this answer obliges, and none of it is optional:**
- `S31b`'s focused zoom and `S31c`'s clip both measure against the CONTAINER. Both must be
  re-pointed at the camera's rect.
- ⚠ **`TP-141`'s evidence must be RE-ESTABLISHED, not re-labelled.** Its value was that it counted
  PAINTED PIXELS (`100,908 px red, 0 px green`) rather than positions — a position-based assertion
  passed both before and after the fix it proved. Re-pointing it at a new boundary without
  re-earning that measurement throws away the only check here that could ever fail for the right
  reason.
- `TP-140`'s two assertions are then satisfiable again, and must be re-measured against the camera
  rect — **still not loosened.**
- ⚠ **The furniture is off screen at rest.** This is the cost the option was listed with: the Deck,
  score column and End/Discard/Rules sit at the picture's edges while the camera rests on the middle
  position. `Q39` has the Entrance follow the camera; **nothing yet says what the rest of the HUD
  does**, and this answer does not settle it. If it needs a ruling, raise it as a new gap rather
  than assuming — which is the mistake `S31e` made here.

⚠ **`H20` LANDS FIRST** (owner ruling, `S33` pulled ahead of `S32`). The camera's rect is a function
of how the wall packs the picture, and the picture currently renders squashed to ~1/3 width because
`wall_packer._picture_size()` never reads `design_size.x`. Re-pointing anything at the camera before
the re-pack means calibrating against geometry that is about to move.

## What was found

`S31e` changed `Levels/game_view.tscn` so that `PlayContainer` stretches to fill the game picture:

```
58b223aa: anchors_preset = 0,  offset_left = 423, offset_right = 1142
6ed95277: anchors_preset = -1, anchor_right = 1.0
```

Measured consequence: the board's scroll window spans **x (0.0 .. 3640.476)** of a **3656 px**
picture. **A grid cannot leave a window that wide.** So `TP-140`'s two "the other grids are OUT OF
VIEW while one is focused" assertions are not miscalibrated — they are **structurally
unsatisfiable**, and so is `H22`, independent of any test's rect.

This defeats what `S31b` and `S31c` were built to achieve. `S31c` in particular clipped the scroll
container so that "other grids out of view" was true of the PIXELS, evidenced at the time by
`TP-141` counting **100,908 px red / 0 px green**. Widening the container to the picture removes the
thing that clip was clipping against.

⚠ The change is **undocumented** — it appears in no line of the `S31e` commit message and in no
entry of the handoff's failure inventory. It was found by diffing `game_view.tscn` between the two
commits while investigating why `TP-140` could not be satisfied.

It is **`GAP-021` option (a)** — *"The screen STRETCHES to the picture"* — implemented silently.
`GAP-021`'s answer chose none of (a)-(d) for the furniture; it answered the GRID question (one grid

`S31e` assumed. This file is the raising.

⚠ **A fourth option was checked for before filing** (`GAP-014`'s lesson), and option (d) below is it.

## Options

- **(a) Keep the stretch; give `H22` a different window.** `PlayContainer` stays picture-wide and
  "out of view" comes to mean *outside the CAMERA's rect*, not outside the scroll container. Honest
  about the fact that the camera, not the container, is what a player sees through. Costs: `S31b`'s
  focused zoom and `S31c`'s clip both measure against the container and would need re-pointing, and
  `TP-141`'s painted-pixel evidence would have to be re-established against the new boundary.
- **(b) Revert `PlayContainer` to a window-sized rect that FOLLOWS the camera.** One position wide,
  tracking `pan_grid` — the literal reading of `Q39`'s *"follows the camera, think of it like a
  player hand"* extended from the Entrance to the furniture as a whole. `H22`, `S31b` and `S31c` all
  keep working unchanged. Costs: the furniture must now be driven by the camera every step, and
  `Q13`(d)'s centring becomes a per-position rule — which needs confirming, because `Q13` is
  ⚑contract.
- **(c) Revert to the authored 1152-px offsets and park the spread.** Returns to `58b223aa`'s
  geometry, where `TP-140` passes and `H22` has no step. `GAP-019`/`GAP-021` already declined the
  equivalent twice; listed for completeness.
- **(d) Defer until `H20` has re-packed the wall.** ⚠ **The owner has already pulled `H20`
  (`S33`) ahead of `S32`.** The picture currently renders horizontally squashed to ~1/3 width
  because `wall_packer._picture_size()` derives the packed width from `design_size.y *
  window_aspect` and never reads `design_size.x`. Until the re-pack lands, **what a "window" of the
  picture even is remains undefined** — so answering (a) vs (b) now means answering it against
  geometry that is about to change. Costs: `S31e` stays incomplete and `TP-140` stays red meanwhile.
