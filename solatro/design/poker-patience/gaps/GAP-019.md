# GAP-019 — the game picture is window-aspect, so the camera has no grid step to make

status: resolved
outcome: answered — (e), build the focused zoom first and re-measure
raised: during execution plan step S32 (the camera migration `GAP-016` deferred to Phase 7)
design: DESIGN.md §36 flowchart H (H1, H2, H21, H22, H23), `QR3`, `Q159`, `Q160`; `GAP-016`
severity: CONTRADICTION

## OWNER ANSWER — (e)

**Build the focused zoom first, then re-measure whether the camera has a step to make.**

`GAP-017`'s ruling part 3 — *"goal is that focused grid has height matching viewport height. while
focused, other grids should be out of view"* — is implemented as **`S31b`**, and `TP-105` stays
parked until the geometry has been measured again with the zoom in place. No design node is amended
and nothing built so far is thrown away.

⚠ **`TP-105` IS NOT CANCELLED — it is deferred a second time.** Whoever finishes `S31b` must
re-measure the focused pose against the picture and re-open the camera question with numbers. If
the zoom's own stepping turns out to satisfy `H22`, say so with the measurement; if it does not,
this gap's options (a)–(d) are still on the table.

⚠ **The buffer question was NOT answered.** `grid_buffer_px` stays at 220 and stays in raw pixels,
so at `card_scale` 1.0 the gap between grids remains WIDER than a grid block (220 vs 216). Raise it
again if the zoom makes it look wrong.

## ⚠ OVERSEER ADDITIONS — two things the filing did not have

**(1) `Q166`'s formula is written in units the game no longer uses, and the buffer was never
rescaled.** `DESIGN.md` §1i states its whole table as *"Screen px at `card_scale` = 2.5"*, and that
is where the **540** in `Q166`(a)'s *"3·540 + 2·buffer"* comes from. But `card_scale` **ships at
1.0** (`Scripts/player_settings.gd:27`; it was moved 2.5 -> 1 back in `S20b2`). At 1.0 a grid block
measures **216 px = 540 / 2.5** — which is exactly why the picture came out 1219 px rather than the
~2060 the formula reads like.

⚠ **The buffer did NOT come down with it.** `grid_buffer_px` is 220, so at the shipped scale **the
gap between two grids is WIDER than a grid** (220 vs 216), and that is what makes the grid pitch
444 px and three grids span 1113. At 2.5 scale the same knob was a modest gap beside a 540 px grid.
So the picture is not merely "not several times wider" — **most of its width is buffer.** Whether
`grid_buffer_px` should be expressed in art units and scaled by `card_scale`, like every other
geometry knob in §1i, is a question this gap should answer alongside the rest.

**(2) The camera's step may be blocked by ORDER, not by geometry.** The measurement *"the camera's
focused resting pose sees 98% of the picture"* was taken on a build with **no focused zoom** —
`GAP-017`'s owner ruling part 3, *"focused grid has height matching viewport height … while focused,
other grids should be out of view"*, is **not implemented yet**, and Phase 6 deliberately shipped no
zoom (`GAP-016`=(d)). With that zoom in place the focused pose would see a fraction of the picture,
and *"the camera steps between the 3 grid positions"* is arguably just what that zoom's stepping IS
— `H22` and `GAP-017` part 3 may be the same feature described from two ends. **That makes "build
the focused zoom first, then re-measure" a candidate option (e) below**, and it is the one that
amends no design node.

## What the design says

- **`QR3` = (a)**, verbatim: *"the wall's camera pans over a wide picture — **the game picture
  becomes several times wider than the window**, and the wall camera's resting pose on it becomes a
  family of poses instead of one"*
- **`H1`** "game picture design_size sized for exactly 3 grids"
- **`H2`** "height = the board's natural height or the aspect minimum, whichever is larger"
- **`H21`** "Info mode fits the **window-aspect view, not the whole wide picture**"
- **`H22`** "picture frame holds 3 grid positions; the camera steps between them"
- **`H23`** "camera pans between grid positions; the scroller reveals more of ONE grid"

## What the ANSWER says

`Q160` is an owner override with a free-text note, and it is the source both `H22`/`H23` and §36's
prose restate:

> *"one scroll container inside the picture to deal with tall stacks and potentially infinite grids
> or very large grids. Camera will pan over 3 possible grid positions since that is size of picture
> frame, and user can then choose to further scroll to reveal more of a single grid. choosing to pan
> left and right will shift current view of 3 grids left or right if there are more than 3 grids off
> edge of picture frame."*

It does not settle this. Its two sentences describe **different views**: *"camera will pan over 3
possible grid positions"* needs the frame to be wider than what the camera shows, while *"pan left
and right will shift current view of 3 grids ... if there are MORE THAN 3 grids"* describes all
three visible at once, with panning reachable only past the cap (`Q7` = "cap be 3 for now"). Which
one the picture is sized for is exactly the decision below.

`QR3` = (a) and `Q159` = (b) carry no note; the option text quoted above is all there is.

## What it does not say — measured, on the picture as built

`H2` is implemented faithfully (`PlayArea.game_picture_design_size`), and it has a consequence
nothing in flowchart H states: the aspect minimum sets `height >= width * 9/16`, so **the picture
can never be wider than the window aspect.** Measured at the shipped defaults, window 1152x648:

| fact | measured |
|---|---|
| game picture `design_size` | **1219 x 685** — 1.06x the window width, not "several times" |
| picture aspect vs window aspect | **1.7796 vs 1.7778** — window-aspect by construction |
| camera's focused resting pose sees | **1194 x 672 of 1219 x 685 — 98% of the picture** |
| board's scroll window INSIDE the picture | **719 px** (side panels take the other 500) |
| three grids span | **1113 px**, so the board overflows its window by **394 px** |
| grid pitch | ~444 px, so the frame shows **~1.6 grid positions**, not 3 |

Three things follow, and each contradicts a node:

1. **There is nothing for the camera to step across.** At the resting pose 98% of the picture is
   already on screen; a camera step would pan onto bare frame and wall. `H22`/`H23`'s camera half
   is not implementable on this geometry.
2. **`H21` presupposes a picture wider than window-aspect** — "the window-aspect view, not the
   whole wide picture" is vacuous when the two are the same rectangle.
3. **`H22`'s premise is false as built.** The picture is sized for three grid BLOCKS, but the play
   area spends 500 px of it on side panels, so the frame holds ~1.6 grid positions. The scroller
   has 394 px of genuine horizontal work at the cap — it is not doing the camera's job for want of
   trying, it is doing work no camera on this picture could do.

`GAP-016` obliged Phase 7 to migrate grid-stepping onto the wall camera and warned that option (b)
— the camera never moving during a show — was NOT chosen. **The migration cannot be performed
without first changing how the picture is sized, which is `H1`/`H2`.**

## Why it blocks

Every option changes the picture's size or aspect, which changes the render target, the overview's
"entirely picture" property, Info mode (`H21`), and where `S29`'s cross-grid selection scrolls. They
differ in what the player sees at rest, and reversing one after `S33` is expensive.

## Options

- **(a) Widen the picture to N window-widths and keep the camera literal.** `game_picture_design_size`
  becomes ~3 window widths with a ONE-window-width height, so the frame really holds 3 grid
  positions and the camera steps between three poses. `H21` becomes meaningful. Costs `H2`: the
  zoomed-out view can no longer be entirely picture — pulling back to see all three shows bare wall
  above and below, or crops. The board must also be scaled up to fill a picture 3x its span.
- **(b) Keep S31's picture; the camera's step is a ZOOM-IN family.** The overview pose stays the
  whole picture; focusing grid `i` zooms the camera onto that grid's share of the picture and the
  pan steps `x` between three such poses. `QR3`'s "family of poses" reads naturally, `H2` survives
  untouched, and the scroller keeps only vertical and oversized-grid reveal. Costs sharpness: a ~3x
  zoom magnifies a 1219 px render target, so the render target must be raised (the
  `size_2d_override` mechanism `_apply_design_render_size` already has, used the other way);
  1219x3 = 3657 is still under `game_picture_max_render_px`.
- **(c) Fix the frame first, then decide.** The picture is sized to the board span but must hold the
  board span PLUS the play area's 500 px of side panels for `H22`'s premise to be true at all. Size
  it to the whole screen's width at 3 grids, re-measure, and see whether any camera step remains.
  Cheapest to try, and it is required by (a) and (b) both; it does not by itself produce a camera
  step.
- **(e) BUILD THE FOCUSED ZOOM FIRST, THEN RE-MEASURE.** `GAP-017`'s owner ruling part 3 — *"focused
  grid has height matching viewport height … while focused, other grids should be out of view"* —
  is unbuilt, and every measurement above was taken without it. Implement it, then ask again whether
  the camera has a step to make; `H22`'s "steps between the 3 grid positions" and that zoom's own
  stepping may be one feature seen from two ends. **Amends no design node and throws nothing away**,
  which is why it is listed. Its cost is that `TP-105` stays parked one step longer.
  ⚠ Pairs with a separate decision either way: **should `grid_buffer_px` be expressed in art units
  and scaled by `card_scale`**, like every other geometry number in §1i? At the shipped 1.0 the
  buffer (220) is wider than a grid block (216), and that alone accounts for most of the picture's
  width.
- **(d) The camera steps only past the cap.** At <= 3 grids the frame holds every grid and the
  camera rests; `H24`'s ">3 grids" case is the only one that steps it. Matches `Q160`'s second
  sentence exactly and needs option (c)'s re-sizing to be true, but leaves the camera motionless in
  the shipped game — which is `GAP-016` option (b) arriving by another road, and the owner declined
  that one.
