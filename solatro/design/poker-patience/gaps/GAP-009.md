# GAP-009 — Nothing builds the grid's VIEW, and Phases 5, 6 and 7 all assume it exists

status: open
outcome: —
raised: 2026-08-27, starting Phase 5 (S20 landed; S21 could not)
design: PLAN.md §3 Phase 5 (S20–S25), Phase 6 (S26–S30); NAMES.md §10; TEST_PLAN.md TP-81–TP-96
severity: CONTRADICTION

## What the design says

`NAMES.md` §10 fixes two scene nodes:

| Path | Notes |
|---|---|
| `%GridContainer` | The board root inside `PlayArea`; hosts one child per grid. |
| `%GridPanel` | One per grid. ⚠ **Draws nothing** — the gap defines a grid. It is a positioning node only. |

Phase 5 then states the geometry rules that apply to them — upward stacks, a shared bottom
edge, rows pushed up, height labels above stacks, row/column/special labels around a grid,
cross-grid row alignment — and Phase 6 states how the player moves around them (zoom out, click
a grid, pan between grids, keyboard selection across grids).

## What the repo says

**`PlayArea` does not render grids at all.** Verified: `grids` does not appear anywhere in
`UI/play_area.gd`. The board it builds is exactly the two legacy zones —

```gdscript
set_card_zone(upper_zone_right, game_state.upper_zone_type, game_state.upper_zone)
set_card_zone(lower_zone_right, game_state.lower_zone_type, game_state.lower_zone)
```

— and every geometry helper is keyed on the legacy `Vector3i(zone, col, row)`:
`slot_center_global(v)`, `_row_open_offset(zone_x, row_z)`, `_row_covers_anything(zone_x, row_z)`.
There is no `%GridContainer` and no `%GridPanel` in any scene. A card placed on a grid has no
control, no visual and no on-screen position.

## What it does not say

**Which step builds it.** Walking `PLAN.md` §3 end to end: S20 is a comment re-derivation
(landed); S21–S25 are geometry RULES over an assumed grid view; S26–S30 are camera and input
over an assumed board; S31–S34 are the wall around it. No step's description, and no `TP` id,
covers *"create the container, create one panel per grid, create 25 cell slots per panel, bind
a card visual to a cell, and give `slot_center_global` a grid coordinate to compute"*.

`NAMES.md` names the nodes, which is what a registry does — it fixes what they will be called
if they are built. It does not schedule them, and no step claims them.

## Why it matters

It is the single blocker in front of everything visual, and its absence is invisible from the
suite because every Phase 1–4 and Phase 8 test is headless by design.

- **Phase 5 cannot start past S20.** `TP-81`–`TP-83` measure a stack growing upward in a cell,
  a row's cards sharing a bottom edge, and a tall stack pushing the rows above it, on
  `FIX-STACK-5` / `FIX-MIXED-H`. `TP-84` asserts `slot_center_global` stays pure math *for a
  grid coordinate*. `TP-91`–`TP-94` place labels around a grid. None of it has anything to
  measure. The work is not "invert `_row_open`"; it is "build the thing, then invert it".
- **Phase 6 and Phase 7 sit on top of Phase 5's geometry** (`PLAN.md` §5: *"Phases 5, 6, 7 are
  sequential among themselves — each builds on the last's geometry"*).
- **`S19b` and the props sit behind it too.** `PLAN.md` §4 reserves the suit-prop system to
  *"what `slot_center_global` forces"*, and `slot_center_global` cannot take a grid coordinate
  until there is a grid layout to compute one from. Until then a scored grid line pays its
  points and fires no props (recorded in the handoff's Open bugs).
- ⚠ It is also **the last thing standing between the engine and a playable game.** Everything
  else closes: the deal, the refill, the commitment, grab and place (`GAP-008`), scoring, undo,
  the interrupted-placement replay, the live score, End. A player still cannot SEE the board
  they are playing on.

## Options I can see

- **(a)** a new step immediately before `S21` that builds the grid view and nothing else —
  container, one panel per grid, 25 cell slots, card binding, and `slot_center_global` extended
  to a `BoardCoord` — leaving S21–S25 as the geometry rules they are written as. Keeps every
  existing step's scope intact and gives the phase gate something to measure; costs a step the
  plan has to grow
- **(b)** fold it into `S21`, which already owns the stack geometry — nothing new to schedule,
  but S21 becomes by far the largest step in the plan and its done-when (`TP-81`–`TP-84`) says
  nothing about the thing that would be most of the work
- **(c)** treat it as Phase 6's, since `S26` (*"click a grid to zoom in"*) and `S28` (*"the one
  scroll container"*) already imply drawn grids — but that inverts `PLAN.md` §5's own ordering,
  and Phase 5's geometry tests would then be blocked on a LATER phase
- **(d)** the grid view REPLACES the legacy two-zone rendering rather than sitting beside it —
  the honest end state, since the tableau is gone from `rules1` and the Entrance is the only
  legacy zone the grid game still uses. This is the largest option and it overlaps `S19b`
  substantially, so it may be that (d) and `S19b` are one piece of work

⚠ **Whichever wins, the Entrance is a special case that needs saying out loud.** It is still
`upper_zone` and it IS rendered today, and `S22` puts it at `y == -1` of the grid it is attached
to — so it has to end up positioned by the grid layout while remaining a legacy zone until
`S19b` retires that representation.

**Blast radius** — `UI/play_area.gd` (the board build, `slot_center_global`, `_row_open_offset`,
`_row_covers_anything`), `Levels/game_view.tscn`, `NAMES.md` §10, and the done-when of every
step from `S21` to `S34`.

**Meanwhile** — parked: all of Phase 5 from `S21`, and Phases 6 and 7 behind it. Continued on:
`S20`, which is a comment re-derivation plus a gate and needed no view at all — landed.
