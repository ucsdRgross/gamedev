# GAP-035 — Entrance cards clip at larger `card_scale`; the Entrance joins the board's scroller

status: resolved
outcome: answered by the owner — the Entrance container **grows upward and outward so its cards never
  clip**, it **shares ONE vertical scrollbar with the grids**, and it is **STICKY AT THE BOTTOM** so it
  can never be scrolled out of view
raised: owner observation — Entrance cards get their top half clipped at larger `card_scale`
design: DESIGN.md `Q313`=(a), `Q1`, §28, §36 flowchart H (`H13`); supersedes `S20`'s pinning
severity: CONTENT CLIPPED BY ITS OWN CONTAINER

## OWNER RULING

> *"also noticed an instance of entrance row cards getting top half clipped if card scale is bigger.
> For now lets have it so that container containing entrance cards grows upwards and outwards as well
> so all entrance cards never get clipped, similar to grid. this will raise grid upwards which is
> expected. the entrance and grids will share one vertical scrollbar"*

Plus, on the risk that a shared scrollbar lets the Entrance scroll away:

> **STICKY AT THE BOTTOM.** One shared scrollbar and one shared scroll range, but the Entrance stays
> anchored at the bottom of the viewport and can never be scrolled out of view.

## ⚠ THIS SUPERSEDES `S20`'s PINNING — record it, do not treat it as a violation

`EntranceCardLayer` is currently pinned OUTSIDE the board's scroll *"so its cards can never be
clipped or scrolled away"*. **That pinning is retired.** The guarantee it existed for is preserved by
different means: the container grows instead of clipping, and stickiness keeps the Entrance on screen.

⚠ **The guarantee itself still binds.** The Entrance is the surface the player drags cards FROM, so
"never scrolled out of view" is not decoration — losing it would make the game unplayable while
holding a card.

## What the structure is today — measured

```
PlayArea
├── SmoothScrollContainer            the board's scroller
│   └── TopLevelVBox                 ALIGNMENT_END -- this is the board's FLOOR
│       ├── GridContainer
│       └── CardLayer / PropLayer / ParticleLayer / OverlayLayer
└── EntranceStrip                    a SIBLING, entirely outside that scroller
    └── EntranceHTrack
        └── EntranceVScroll          a SECOND ScrollContainer
            └── EntranceContent
                ├── UpperZone (HSplitContainer -> UpperZoneLeft / UpperZoneRight)
                └── EntranceCardLayer
```

Two scroll contexts, two vertical scrollbars. The ruling collapses them to one.

## ⚠ `Q313`=(a) ALREADY POINTS HERE — this completes it, it does not contradict it

> `Q313`=(a): *"the Entrance is row −1 of the lattice (your `Q1` answer) and its height participates
> in exactly the same offset sum"*

and `Q1`'s free text: *"special case for entrance since it is technically row -1"*. So **"this will
raise grid upwards which is expected" is `Q313`=(a) working**, not a new behaviour. `U38` (§28) says
the same: *"The Entrance itself holds a stack, pushing the grids up."*

## ⚠ IT LIKELY SUBSUMES `GAP-032`'s MECHANISM

`GAP-032` was implemented by putting `board_zoom` on `%EntranceVScroll` via
`_apply_entrance_zoom_rect()`. **If the Entrance moves inside `SmoothScrollContainer`, it inherits
`scroll_container.scale = Vector2.ONE * board_zoom` for free and that mechanism becomes dead code.**
`GAP-032`'s RULING still stands — Entrance and grid scale together — but its implementation may be
deleted here. ⚠ **Check before deleting**: the shared accessor `entrance_strip_height_px()` and the
focused-zoom fixed-point fix are separately earned and should survive.

## ⚠ Traps

- **`TopLevelVBox`'s `ALIGNMENT_END` is the board's FLOOR.** Adding the Entrance as a row must not
  disturb it. ⚠ Standing rule: *"`custom_minimum_size` IS A FLOOR, NOT A CAP"* — a `VBoxContainer` is
  `max(own minimum, sum of children + separations)`, so surplus children push a bottom-aligned
  gutter's rows upward and the error ACCUMULATES.
- **`H13` says ONE scroll container** *"inside the picture, for tall stacks and oversized grids"* —
  this ruling is that node finally being true.
- ⚠ **The known ~11-frame dead-click window on Entrance cards** (*"the card is easing out from under
  the cursor"*) runs through this code. Re-check it; do not assume.
- ⚠ **Horizontal**: the Entrance currently tracks the board's pan through `_sync_entrance_x` /
  `entrance_h_track`. Confirm what happens to that alignment once the Entrance is inside the scroller.
