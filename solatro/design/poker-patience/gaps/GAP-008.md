# GAP-008 — The grid game has no player placement path: nothing answers grab or place

status: resolved
outcome: answered
raised: 2026-08-27, during execution plan step S19 (the rebuild)
design: DESIGN.md chart A (A2–A9, A14), §1f; PLAN.md §1.9; NAMES.md §5 (`TypeGridCell`, `TypeInput`)
severity: CONTRADICTION

## OWNER ANSWER — (a): both rules live on the ZONE TYPE cards

Owner, verbatim:

> *"input zone allows grabbing cards from it regardless of stack.
> can always place on a grid zone cell by default
> these are both rules to adjust for old input zone cards, not effects that come from the
> rules deck. I think this would have been the upper and lower input zone classes"*

The rules this produces:

- **`TypeInput.on_can_grab_stack`** — any card held in that header's Entrance slot may be
  picked up, **regardless of what is stacked on top of it**, and it grabs that one card only.
  Deliberately NOT symmetric with `TypeInput.on_can_place_stack`, which does still require the
  target to be topmost: dropping ONTO the Entrance is stacking, picking up out of it is not.
- **`TypeGridCell.on_can_place_stack`** — a cell **always** accepts, by default.
- ⚠ **NEITHER IS A RULES-DECK CARD.** The owner is explicit that these adjust the old input
  ZONE classes, not the retired `SkillGrabberOgLower` / `SkillPlacerOgLower`. That is a
  contract, not a filing preference: a rules-deck card can be removed from a deck, and a deck
  that had lost its placer would be one the player cannot place from at all. `DESIGN.md` §1f's
  *"grab and place are rules-card decisions"* is therefore true of the TABLEAU's rules and not
  of these — the zone furniture carries them now.
- So option **(a)**, and no new `class_name` or art frame is needed.

### What "always accepts" means for stacking, which chart A6/A7 also rules on

Chart A says *"occupied cells refuse: stacking is effect-only"*. That still holds, and it
needs no second rule: **an occupied cell does not present its zone card as the drop target.**
It presents the card sitting on top of it — the same convention
`Game._no_held_card_has_a_legal_placement` already uses. Nothing answers `on_can_place_stack`
for a played card, so a hand-drop onto an occupied cell is refused by there being no rule at
all, exactly as §1f says refusal is achieved.

### The mechanical half, also now built

`Game.try_place` gained the grid branch this gap flagged as unowned: when the target resolves
to a cell zone card (`GameData.cell_type_coord`), the drop routes to `place_card_in_grid`
rather than the legacy move engine — so it lifts the card out of the Entrance, fires the
mutation broadcast the detector listens to, refills, and commits its own undo step.

### What this unblocked

`Tests/Interaction/test_interaction.gd::test_rebuild_leaves_no_dead_controls` is UNPARKED —
its five checks had been silently not running. `AcceptEmptyCellsOfOneGrid`, the test double
standing in for the real rule, is retired: a mock that shadows the thing it stands in for
keeps passing after that thing is deleted.

⚠ Still parked, on `GAP-003` and not on this: a scored grid line spawns no props, and no act
on a grid board outlives two frames.

## What the design says

Chart A is the whole player loop, and it routes a placement through the existing dispatch:

```
A2 player picks any of the 5, any order
A3 click-to-grab or drag, both supported
A4 Game.try_grab asks on_can_grab_stack
A5 NEW legal cells highlighted while a card is held
A6 target cell empty?  -> A7 occupied cells refuse
A8 Game.try_place asks on_can_place_stack
A9 Board.place_card commits, state.revision bumps once
A14 put the card back costs nothing, OK_NOOP path
```

`DESIGN.md` §1f fixes the principle: *"Grab and place are rules-card decisions, not hard-coded."*
It also names every implementer that exists:

> *"The only implementers are `SkillGrabberOgLower` / `SkillPlacerOgLower` (the Klondike run rule)
> and `TypeInput` (`on_can_place_stack`: you may drop onto an Entrance header when it is topmost)."*

And `Q208`=(a|b) removes **both** of those cards from `rules1` at S19.

## What the repo says

Verified by grep over `Cards/`, `Scripts/`, `Levels/` (tests excluded):

| hook | implementers today |
|---|---|
| `on_can_grab_stack` | `SkillGrabberOgLower` only — **removed at S19** |
| `on_can_place_stack` | `SkillPlacerOgLower` (**removed at S19**), `TypeInput` (drop *onto* the Entrance) |

`TypeGridCell` (`Cards/Types/type_grid_cell.gd`) implements neither. After S19 removes the two
Og cards, **no card answers either hook for a grid cell**, so `try_grab` returns `[]` for every
Entrance card and `try_place` returns `false` for every cell. Chart A's A4 and A8 both dead-end.

There is a second half to the same hole. Even if a card said yes, `Game.try_place` commits through
the **legacy** move path only:

```gdscript
# Levels/game.gd:294-297
for moving_data in stacked:
    move_data_ontop_data(moving_data, onto_data, 1, false)   # -> find_data_vec3(dest)
```

`find_data_vec3` reads the legacy `Vector3i` index, which holds no grid cells, so it returns
`Vector3i.MIN` for a `TypeGridCell` card. `Game.place_card_in_grid()` — the function that
actually commits into a cell, sets `committed_grid`, broadcasts and refills — is called from
**tests only** (verified: 30 call sites, all under `Tests/`). Nothing in `try_place` reaches it.

⚠ §1f's *"a placed card cannot be moved or stacked on is achieved by REMOVING rules cards"* is
about **board-to-board** moves (`Q21`=b), and that part is correct and deliberate. It does not
cover Entrance-to-cell, which chart A requires.

## What it does not say

No document names **which card answers `on_can_grab_stack` and `on_can_place_stack` in the grid
game**, nor how `try_place` reaches `place_card_in_grid`. `NAMES.md` §5 lists `TypeGridCell`
purely as *"The per-cell zone card"* and pins `TypeInput` as *"Unchanged except `on_next` is
removed"* — which reads as forbidding a new handler on it. No plan step between S1 and S37 owns
this: S15/S16 build the grid cards, S17/S18 build the Entrance and the commit, S19 removes the
tableau. Chart A's A4, A5 and A8 trace to **no step and no TP id** in `TEST_PLAN.md` (A9 traces to
TP-10, which tests `Board.place_in_cell` directly, never through `try_place`).

## Why it matters

This is the game's only input verb. Without it the grid game cannot be played at all — the
Entrance deals, refills and commits, and the player can do nothing with the cards.

It also blocks work already in this run's range:

- **S19** — `Tests/Interaction/test_interaction.gd`'s drag-to-place checks and
  `Tests/UI/test_visual_layers.gd`'s held-card layering exercise `try_grab`/`try_place`
  specifically. They cannot be rebuilt onto the grid game against a dispatch that answers no.
- **S35** — `TP-123` *"A put-it-back (no-op) placement commits no snapshot"* is chart A's A14
  cancel path, which only exists through `try_grab`/`try_place`. `TP-121`'s *"every placement"*
  is ambiguous the same way: engine placements (`place_card_in_grid`) already commit.

Picking a shape now is expensive to reverse: the highlight (A5), the legal-cell query
(`_no_held_card_has_a_legal_placement`, which already dispatches `on_can_place_stack` and today
is trivially true because nothing answers), the view's grab affordance, and every rebuilt suite
all bind to whatever answers.

## Options I can see

- **(a)** `TypeGridCell` answers `on_can_place_stack` (yes when its own cell is empty, no when
  occupied — chart A's A6/A7 exactly), and `TypeInput` gains `on_can_grab_stack` (yes for the
  topmost card of its own Entrance slot) — mirrors precisely how `TypeInput` already answers
  `on_can_place_stack` for the Entrance, needs **no new class and no new art frame**, and puts
  each rule on the zone card that owns the square it governs; but it contradicts `NAMES.md`'s
  *"`TypeInput` unchanged except `on_next` is removed"*, and it makes grab/place properties of
  the **board furniture** rather than of a rules card the player could lose or swap
- **(b)** two new rules cards in `rules1` — a grid grabber and a grid placer, direct
  replacements for `SkillGrabberOgLower` / `SkillPlacerOgLower` — keeps §1f's *"rules-card
  decisions"* literally true, keeps them swappable/removable content, and matches the shape the
  archived pair had; but it needs **two new `class_name`s and two art frames that `NAMES.md` §5
  does not claim** (9–13 are taken), and two cards whose removal silently makes the game
  unplayable
- **(c)** one new rules card covering both hooks — same as (b) at half the cost, one
  `class_name`, one frame; but it fuses two rules the old design deliberately kept as separate
  cards
- **(d)** hard-code both in `Game.try_grab` / `try_place` for the grid, leaving the dispatch for
  content to extend — smallest change, no naming question at all; but it directly contradicts
  §1f, and §1f's *"with no implementer the answer is already you cannot"* guarantee for
  board-to-board moves then has to be re-established some other way

⚠ **Whichever wins, `try_place` needs a grid branch** that routes to `place_card_in_grid` when
the target is a `TypeGridCell`, so the commit sets `committed_grid`, broadcasts
`on_board_mutated` / `on_card_placed`, and refills. That part is mechanical and not itself a
question — but it is not written, and no step owns it either.
