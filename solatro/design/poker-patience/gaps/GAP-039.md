## OWNER NOTE

> *"note that if entrance is not snapped to a grid, it should smoothly transition between them.
> Shouldn't really be an issue though since entrance should be tied to camera or view looking at each
> grid first."*

So: **the requirement is smooth motion between grids, and the expectation is that it comes for free**
because the Entrance follows the camera/view rather than being positioned independently.

## What to check

`_sync_entrance_x()` slides `entrance_h_track` in X to mirror the board's horizontal scroll, and the
Entrance is a SIBLING of the board's scroller rather than a child of it. So its horizontal position is
DERIVED each frame from the board's own pan.

⚠ **A derived-every-frame value is exactly what makes this smooth for free** — and the standing rule
records why that shape matters: *"A VALUE SAFE TO READ PER-FRAME IS NOT SAFE TO CAPTURE ONCE.
`_sync_entrance_x` survives a fallback it re-derives every frame; a latched copy was poisoned
forever."*

**Confirm rather than assume**: watch the Entrance during a camera step between grids and report
whether it moves continuously with the board or jumps. ⚠ **A STILL FRAME IS THE WRONG INSTRUMENT FOR
ANYTHING WITH A DURATION** — run it and report what MOVED, do not screenshot it.
