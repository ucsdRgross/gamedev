Every tile is a variation of the palette you have — the first one is the palette you have. Click any tile to take it; the next dozen are then variations of that. Ten clicks will get you somewhere no amount of slider-dragging will. Each tile shows the palette's context colour-space maps — everything, sprites, scenery, sky, UI and FX, each at full saturation — so you are comparing whole palettes in every job they do, not one scene's worth of slots.

Hover to read a colour, click to copy its hex. A map's position always means the same thing but may not reach every colour; a layout shows every colour but moves them when the palette changes.

Every reference image in the generated palette. Drop PNG, JPEG or GIF files anywhere here to add your own — animations are recoloured in full and play back at their own speed. Running without the local server, so added images last for this session only.
